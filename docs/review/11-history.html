A colour history is viable, and it is blocked on something that does not exist

You are right that this is the one screen where colour would carry information. Two things to know before choosing it.

Contrast is not the obstacle. All four quadrant inks clear the 4.5:1 text floor in both faces:

The obstacle is that no mapping exists. A row’s title comes from friendlyTitle, whose vocabulary is six presets — Focus, Energize, Calm, Unwind, Uplift, Intense — plus three bio bands and a generic. Nothing anywhere in the codebase maps any of those to a quadrant. I searched for it rather than assuming. So picking colour here is not just a design choice: it commissions a mapping, and that mapping is an interpretive claim about what someone was feeling. I have drawn one below so you can see it, and every row is labelled as proposed — I am not going to quietly invent the semantics of the archive.

Three things I previously told you were wrong here, and are not
Old claimChecked
“Row not tappable”HistoryRow is an AnimatedPressable with accessibilityRole="button", an onPress, and a press-scale animation. The whole row is one tap.
“Two competing buttons per row”The shipped row has a medallion, a title, a meta line and a time. No buttons at all. The board matches — one chevron, no action pair.
“Three strings the product cannot produce”Half right. metaLine() emits exactly Live · Running / Manual, so no track-count segment exists — but trackCount is a field on SessionItem. The data is there; the meta line just does not compose it. That is a design choice available to you, not a fiction.
What else is wrong

The board drops the medallion, and with it the one distinction the code already draws. SourceGlyph tints accent.glow for a live session and content.secondary for a manual one — so live-vs-manual is visible at a glance in the app and invisible on the board.

The title column mixes two vocabularies. “Unwind” and “Peak Energy” come from friendlyTitle’s fixed maps. “Resting but content” does not — it reads like a state-vector sentence of the kind Now Playing and the Receipt use. A backend-supplied title could legitimately produce it, so I am not calling it fabricated; but one column showing both preset nouns and state prose makes the archive look like two different products.
