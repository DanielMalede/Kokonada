Screen 17 — Tokens

The last board. A token sheet's only job is to be true — a scale nothing obeys is decoration. So the three treatments differ in how much truth-checking the board does about itself.

What changed since you last saw this material

  • Most of the old token conflicts are already resolved on the existing board. --inkbtn, --glow, --emo and --sky are declared retired; --line (1.34:1) and --line2 (1.85:1) were measured, found under the 3:1 floor, and demoted to decorative separators with --track taking every boundary that matters.
  • The breath conflict is gone. The canvas now paints 4200ms in fourteen places, matching shipped motion.duration.breath = 4200. The old 4600 (which is focalGlow, a different role) is no longer used as a breath anywhere.
  • The board declares flow · 15000 as "Reserved — unused in this direction". That is true: nothing on the canvas runs 15000ms. But the thing flow exists for — the ambient living field — is running, on six boards, at an undeclared 5670ms. See below; this is the finding I care most about.
  • I counted every duration the canvas paints against the shipped five-value scale. Ten values are off-scale — 80, 160, 320, 380, 400, 480, 560, 760, 2600 and 5670. The scale is currently aspirational rather than descriptive.
  • --inkbtn is declared retired on the board and is still painted by Onboarding.dc.html. One live counter-example to the board's own claim.

What I'm unsure about

  • Whether the ambient field should take flow or get its own token. flow: 15000 is shipped and unused; 5670ms is used six times and undeclared. Retuning flow to 5670 makes one token real — but 15000 may be shipped for a drift the canvas has not drawn yet, and I could not establish which. Decision field.
  • Whether a token board should show what it does not yet have. settle: 1200 and a low-power duration are both owed (they have dev items) and neither exists in tokens.ts. Showing them makes the board a plan; hiding them makes it a mirror. I lean toward showing, clearly marked — same argument as the error gap on States.
  • The ten off-scale durations may not all be defects. 1400ms is nine of them and it is genuinely backed — it is SKELETON_PULSE's period, just not in the duration scale. The other nine I cannot defend, but I have not traced each to its origin, so I have listed rather than condemned them.
  • Colour is the one category I did not re-audit here. It was proved centralised by mutation earlier in this pass — one source hue changed, 370 paints moved, nothing left behind — and I have not re-run that since. It is a claim resting on an earlier measurement, not a fresh one.

Treatment A — by category

The catalogue the board is today: colour, type, space, radius, motion, haptics. Complete, well-organised, and it does not check itself.

Treatment B — declared vs painted

Every token beside what the canvas actually paints, and a verdict. Argues that a token sheet which cannot be checked is not a contract.

Treatment C — by decision

Only the tokens where a choice was made, reversed, or is still owed — each with its reason. A ledger, not a catalogue. Small on purpose.
