What is actually wrong

The plate’s axis labels fail contrast in the light theme: 3.50:1 against a 4.5:1 floor. They are --ink3 at 80% opacity. This is the same defect you caught on Onboarding, where the fix was to fade with --ink instead of the already-pale --ink3. That fix was applied there and never carried here. Dark passes at 5.59:1, so it fails in exactly one face — which is why looking at one theme would have missed it.

The instrument is smaller than its own control panel. Measured region heights: the plate stage gets 237px (28%); the read card, chips and prompt below it get 375px (44%). The plate itself renders at 202px on a 390px-wide screen.

The selected activity chip contradicts the shipped component in three ways at once. The board draws it as a flat --accent fill — the emotion hue — on a control that has nothing to do with emotion. ActivityChips.tsx:19-21 draws a neutral-aurora→gold #F5B93A gradient with #241B45 ink, and states the rule in the file: “Distinction is never colour alone — accent outline + gradient + accessibilityState.selected.” The board distinguishes by fill alone, loses the gold signature, and lets an activity borrow the fused state’s colour.

What I want your eye on

The axis labels are still outside the plate here. On Onboarding you asked for them inside and faded, and said it would look more settled. I have not assumed that carries — it is row one, with the corrected contrast either way.

The re-tint moment is not on this screen yet. You asked that the dots re-tinting to the fused accent be “a moment, not a repaint”, with a real duration and easing token and a reduced-motion still. That is motion, so it cannot be judged in a static frame; it belongs on the Flow board and I will bring it there rather than fake it here.

The text alternative exists and is better than I implied on Onboarding. EmotionListSelector.tsx ships four quadrants with a label, a plain-language descriptor and a distinct glyph each — Calm ◐ settled and easy, Joyful ☀ bright and lifted, Intense ◆ charged and strong, Reflective ☾ quiet and inward. Never colour alone. The board’s “Words” control is the entry point to it.
