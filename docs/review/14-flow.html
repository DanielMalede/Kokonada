What is actually wrong

Transition 00 describes a direction the system does not have. The word cool appears four times: the dots “cool to the fused state”, frame 1 is captioned mid-cool, and “Cooling before the answer exists would be theatre.” That is the vocabulary of the cooler-on-arousal ramp you struck out — “the regulator trigger is distress, not arousal.” The mechanism is not directional: the dots move from the placed hue to the fused hue, and that can be warmer as easily as cooler. Place something bleak, have your body report that you are fine, and the dots warm. The board’s own example happens to cool, which is exactly how a specific case gets written up as a general rule.

Transition 02 contradicts Transition 03, on the same board. T02 says that on arriving at Now Playing the field’s “hue moves violet → sky over 1200 ms … the set beginning to bring the pulse down, made visible.” T03 says a field state change “is never triggered by a single reading. The state must hold for two consecutive reads before the field moves.” Both cannot be true. Either the field re-tints at the cut — before any second read exists, on the strength of a set that has played for zero seconds — or it holds until the body actually changes. T02 is also the more troubling of the two: it shows the user their state improving because the music started, not because they did.

Nothing here covers the two pushed screens. Four transitions cover Generate → Genesis → Now Playing and the field. Neither receipt nor vault appears anywhere on the board. Both are now pushed screens with a back affordance and no tab bar — the Receipt by §7b, the Vault by your screen-13 decision — and a pushed screen’s open-and-dismiss is precisely the kind of thing a flow board exists to specify. Right now two screens can be entered and left with no designed behaviour, which is how three different answers get invented at build time.

What is right, and worth not breaking

Every duration on this board is a real token except one, and that one is honestly labelled. 240 is base, 420 is slow, 120 is fast, and cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) is easing.calm verbatim. 1200 has no token, and the board says so: “Proposed token: motion.duration.settle = 1200.” That is the right way to write down a value the system does not have yet.

Every transition carries a designed reduced-motion still, and one of them states the principle better than I would have: “the amber frame is never shown, because a still of a transition is a lie about what happened.” The degradation ladder ends by keeping Genesis moving longest, “because it is the only place motion is carrying meaning rather than atmosphere.” None of that should change.
