What is actually wrong — measured against §7b

The spec forbids, in words, precisely what the board draws. §7b: “a ‘ceiling’ is an exclusion and must never be drawn as an attainment”, and names the component: CeilingBar — “a limit drawn as a limit, never a progress fill”. The board draws four identical left-anchored attainment fills. Two of them are ceilings. A tempo ceiling of 96 rendered as a 30%-full bar reads as “you are 30% of the way to something”, when it means “nothing above this line was allowed in”.

The tempo bar has no denominator. The other three rows are 0–1 values mapped to percent, which is coherent. Tempo is 96 BPM drawn at 30% — which implies a scale that tops out at 320 BPM. There is no axis, no maximum, and no unit on the bar. My earlier note said “96 → 52%”; the current board says 30%. The number changed, the defect did not.

The verdict head shows a sentence the app cannot currently deliver. The backend emits it — receipt.why = targets.explain.trim() (biometricHandler.js:176), and it is carefully guarded: only present when every axis its sentence claims carried real evidence, and withheld entirely on the favourites path. But the mobile sanitizeReceipt keeps label, detail and caption only — why is dropped on the floor. §7b already flags this as must-fix-before-ship, and it is still true today.

The closing line is not the one the spec mandates. §7b gives it verbatim:

“Nothing here left your phone unencrypted. The engine sees the state name and the targets — never the raw readings.”

The board draws a longer, different line about a coarse intensity band, mood and genre tags. These are not stylistic variants — they make different factual claims about what the AI provider receives. One of them has to change, and I do not think it should be settled by whichever was typed last. My read is that the board’s version is the more accurate one and the spec line is the one to update, but that is a compliance question, not a design one. Row three puts both in front of you.

One state of five, and the mark is hand-drawn again. §7b lists loaded · loading (skeleton, never a spinner) · partial (trajectory absent) · offline · stale. The board draws loaded. The Up-next header also redraws the Spotify logo rather than using the official asset, same as Now Playing. The Up-next rows correctly carry no tap affordance, which §7b explicitly requires — that one is right.
