04 · Connect

Three treatments, both themes, seven independent rows, live assembly at the top. This is the first screen where the achromatic rule and the shipped code actually disagree, so that disagreement is the first decision below.

What I found on this screen

The board inverts the one ethical decision this screen makes. It draws “Continue with mood only” as a filled accent button. The shipped MoodOnlyBar.tsx deliberately draws it as an outline, and says why in the file:

“not resolved → ‘Continue with mood only’ as a confident glow-OUTLINE secondary (an equal, legitimate choice — a filled brand CTA here would nudge users PAST connecting).”

The canvas takes an anti-dark-pattern stance the code went out of its way to encode and reverses it. This is the defect on this screen, and it is not cosmetic.

It also paints the undeclared blue. Enumerated from computed styles, that same button paints #3A5CCC (chroma 146) in light and #9DB4FF (chroma 98) in dark, across five properties. Neither is a token from tokens.ts.

Copy has drifted from the shipped screen almost everywhere. Title is “Connect what you have.” — shipped is “Set up your sound.” under a KOKONADA wordmark. The second card is headed “Your body” — shipped is “Wearable & Health”. Both card captions are missing entirely (“Where your soundtrack plays.” / “Let your body shape the music. Optional, always.”). The status words are caps-mono UNAVAILABLE / NOT YET AVAILABLE; shipped STATUS_WORD is sentence case — Unavailable / Not yet available. And the NEVER A GATE eyebrow appears nowhere in the code; it is invented.

The live action is drawn in the wrong place. The board hangs a small “Connect” pill on the wearable row. The shipped screen makes it a full-width filled “Connect a wearable” inside the card — described in the source as “the protagonist: the ONE live filled CTA”. On a screen where exactly one of three providers can be connected, that distinction is the whole hierarchy.

Health Connect is Android-only and the board never says so. ConnectServicesScreen.tsx:145 raises “Connecting a wearable uses Health Connect, available on Android.” — but only after a tap, on a screen the user may have reached from an iPhone.

Correcting the record twice, in the other direction. My earlier plan said Connect clipped its CTA and mood-only path off the frame (~939px into 844). I measured it: nothing is clipped, in either theme — that was fixed in Stage 2 and I should not have carried it forward. And the board's isIntended chip, which hides the aspirational Connect pills and the REQUIRED eyebrow behind a flag, is good practice: it keeps the end-state visible without letting it be mistaken for today. That stays.

The decision I cannot make for you

Connect is a pre-emotion screen, and the shipped code paints a brand accent on it. Your standing rule is that Splash, Onboarding, Sign in and Connect are achromatic — no accent before an emotion exists. But MoodOnlyBar and the wearable CTA both use the fixed brand accent (accent.glow, accent.glowInk), and the source is explicit that this is never the reactive emotionAccent. So the two are not the same colour doing the same job: one is the emotion, the other is the brand.

That is a genuine fork, not an oversight, so it is row one. What I can tell you is what each option measures — I computed these against the real token values rather than trusting the comment:

PairLightDarkFloor
accent.glow as a label3.65 : 15.71 : 14.5 (text)
accent.glow as a 1.5px ring3.65 : 15.71 : 13.0 (non-text)
content.primary label on base16.74 : 116.33 : 14.5
content.secondary on raised8.23 : 111.13 : 14.5
onAccent on accent.glowInk fill6.62 : 15.71 : 14.5

This reproduces the claim in MoodOnlyBar.tsx exactly: the violet cannot carry the label in the light theme, but it can carry the ring. So the shipped arrangement — accent on the 1.5px boundary, content.primary on the word — is the only way the outline treatment passes. If you rule fully achromatic, the ring becomes --track and nothing is lost but the brand cue.

What I would not do either way: make the mood-only button a filled accent. That option is on the row so you can see it, and it is labelled as what it is.
