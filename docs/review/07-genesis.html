Four things I previously told you were wrong here, and are not

The board was rebuilt in an earlier stage and most of my plan’s complaints no longer describe it. One of these was a plain misreading on my part and I want to name it exactly.

Old claimChecked
“Fabricated trace in the real-data dialect”There is no trace on the board. Three breathing rings and a meter, nothing else.
“dasharray 340 vs a ~287px path”No dasharray anywhere in the file.
“3-tick meter over a ~220s ceiling”The ceiling is 30s — AUTO_SETTLE_MS = 30_000 and GENERATION_TIMEOUT_MS = 30_000 agree. My “220s” came from biometricHandler.js:1437, which is the heart-rate validation range, 30–220 bpm. I read a bpm range as seconds.
“Arousal answered with more light — the ethic inverted”heat() ramps cyan→coral and is capped there, explicitly never reaching RED. And emotionAnchors is entirely cool-band now — sky, periwinkle, violet, indigo — with a never-red VALUE invariant pinned in tokens.test.ts.
What is actually wrong

It draws a determinate three-step meter over a process that has no steps. generationStatusStore holds exactly generating: boolean and an optional message. There is no stage, no index, no percentage — nothing that could fill tick two of three. The board also announces it: role="progressbar" aria-valuenow="2" aria-valuemin="1" aria-valuemax="3", so a screen reader is told “step 2 of 3” of a process with one state.

This is the same problem you already ruled on at Splash, where you chose the breathing dot and said why: “it’s indeterminate, so it’s honest at any duration.” The honest duration range here is 0 to 30 seconds — often under a second, occasionally the full ceiling. A three-step meter has to lie at both ends.

The copy is invented. “Sitting with the shape of it.” appears nowhere in the codebase. The shipped strings are ANALYZING_COPY = 'Reading your signal…' and RESOLVED_COPY = 'Found your sound' — two states, and the second one is the moment the whole product is for.

Two different visuals exist for one moment and nothing says which survives. The board draws three concentric breathing rings. The app ships NeuralAnalysisLoader — a Skia node sphere on a golden-angle Fibonacci distribution with a depth-cued heat ramp, already unit-tested and already NaN-guarded. That is a real component doing a real job; the rings are a drawing. Row two puts them side by side.

A code-hygiene item, not a design one

neuralLoaderMath.ts exports its ramp anchors as CYAN, CORAL and RED, annotated #31E1C4, #FF8A73, #FF5A5A. Those values are stale: they now resolve to #3FB4F0, #8B6FE8 and #4B6FD0 — sky, violet, indigo. The code is correct, because it derives from emotionAnchors rather than restating them. But the names and the comments describe a warm ramp that the values are not, on the one file where someone reasoning about the never-red ethic would look first. A future edit that “restored” the names’ meaning would break the ethic and read as a fix. Worth renaming; I have added it to the colour dev item rather than treating it as canvas work.
