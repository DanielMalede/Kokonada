### CMP-141 · The Design Lab is absent from every uploaded build
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No artifact uploaded to Play Console (any track, internal app sharing included) or App Store Connect (TestFlight included) contains Design Lab code, entry, sentinel, activity or resources; absent, not unreachable. Named hazards: (1) the `ENTRY_FILE` environment variable, which the RN Gradle plugin applies to every non-debuggable Android variant (proven, RED9) and which `react-native-xcode.sh` honours for iOS, including from `ios/.xcode.env.local`; both build paths must refuse it; (2) an edit to `react { entryFile | root | bundleConfig | bundleAssetName | extraPackagerArgs }`; (3) uploading any artifact that was not scanned. No iOS archive is exported for upload, and nothing goes to TestFlight or App Store Connect, until the store-gate PR is merged and the IPA scan exits 0.
- source: Apple App Review Guidelines 2.3.1(a): "Don't include any hidden, dormant, or undocumented features in your app; your app's functionality should be clear to end users and App Review. All new features, functionality, and product changes must be described with specificity in the Notes for Review section of App Store Connect (generic descriptions will be rejected) and accessible for review." 2.3.1(b): "Egregious or repeated behavior is grounds for removal from the Apple Developer Program." 2.2: "Any app submitted for beta distribution via TestFlight should be intended for public distribution and should comply with the App Review Guidelines." https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ (no date on the page). Google Play Deceptive Behavior: "Your app's functionality should be reasonably clear to users; don't include any hidden, dormant, or undocumented features within your app." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888077. Play testing tracks: "Internal tests might not be subject to standard Play policy or security reviews." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334 · retrieved 2026-10-04
- provider: Apple, Google Play
- applies to: F4, the lab, every release
- surfaces: `mobile/KokonadaHealth/index.lab.js`, `src/design/lab/`, `android/app/src/lab/`, the `lab` build type and its refusals in `android/app/build.gradle`, and `scripts/lab/`
- verify at: CI on every PR, `node scripts/lab/assertLabExcluded.mjs` (the import closure, and the release Metro + Hermes bundle with its positive control); CI on Android-affecting PRs, `node scripts/lab/assertManifests.mjs`, the release-classes scan with the lab-classes positive control, and the scan of the real release task's own outputs (`createBundleReleaseJsAndAssets`); before every upload, the exact-artifact scan `node scripts/lab/assertLabExcluded.mjs --scan <file>` in the pre-upload checklists (`docs/store/play-health-connect-declaration.md` §6, `docs/PRIVACY_DECLARATIONS.md`); and for iOS, the store-gate PR's bundle-phase guard
