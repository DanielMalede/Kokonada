### CMP-108 · (Later, iOS) Accuracy claims and the doctor reminder
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: No accuracy claim about a health measurement appears without its method. The app reminds users to check with a doctor.
- source: App Store Review Guidelines 1.4.1: "Apps must clearly disclose data and methodology to support accuracy claims relating to health measurements…"; "Apps should remind users to check with a doctor in addition to using the app and before making medical decisions." · retrieved 2026-09-29
