### REG-018 · Annex III high-risk classification and its date
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Counsel decides whether the system falls in Annex III point 1(c), "AI systems intended to be used for emotion recognition". Such systems "shall be considered to be high-risk" (Art. 6(2)).
  - If so, the Chapter III Sections 1–3 regime applies from 2 Dec 2027.
- source: Annex III point 1(c) and Art. 6(2) [K1] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION).
