### REG-017 · No message triggered by an inferred state
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No notification, prompt, rating request, offer or re-engagement message is triggered, timed or worded by an inferred state, and in particular not by a distress-class state.
- source: AI Act Art. 5(1)(a) and (b) [K1] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION). UCPD Art. 9(c) ("the exploitation by the trader of any specific misfortune or circumstance of such gravity as to impair the consumer's judgement…") [K13] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION)
- jurisdiction: EU
- classification assumption: every option. The text does not ban every such message, so this is a line the owner draws for the build.
- applies to: P1, P2, P9; backend (the `User.pushTokens` field exists)
- surfaces: notification preferences; any future push
