### CMP-095 · Title and metadata rules
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The title is 30 characters or fewer. Title, icon and developer name carry no emoji, ALL CAPS (unless it is the brand), ranking or award claims, price or promo text, or misleading comparisons with other brands.
- source: Google Play Metadata: "Your app title must be 30 characters or less"; examples prohibited: "'App of the year,' '#1,' 'Best of Play'", "'10% off,' 'free for limited time only'", and "Misleading comparisons to other brands or apps without authorization". https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842 · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Google Play
- applies to: P13, store listing
- surfaces: listing draft
- verify at: store submission
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: none
