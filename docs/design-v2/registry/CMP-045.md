### CMP-045 · YouTube data is kept for 30 days at most
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: YouTube data the user authorised (liked videos, titles, channels, and taste derived from them) is kept for 30 calendar days at most, then deleted or refreshed.
- source: YouTube API Developer Policies III.E.4.c: "API Clients may store all other types of Authorized Data … for no longer than 30 calendar days". https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies · retrieved 2026-09-29 · Amended 2026-09-30: re-verified on the page "Last updated 2026-09-14 UTC".
