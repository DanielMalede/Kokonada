### CMP-099 · Official Sign in with Google button
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The Google button uses exactly the published theme: Light #FFFFFF fill, #747775 1-px inside stroke, #1F1F1F text; Dark #131314 / #8E918F / #E3E3E3; or Neutral #F2F2F2 with no stroke and #1F1F1F text. The "G" is the standard colour version on white, and the label is Google Sans Medium 14/20. The alternative is Google's pre-approved button files. These values are fixed and never theme tokens. · Amended 2026-09-30: Google Sans is not licensed to Kokonada, so for the current app only the pre-approved files comply.
- source: Sign in with Google branding guidelines (updated 2026-07-07): "Your website or app must follow these guidelines to complete the app verification process"; the colour table; "The button font is Google Sans Medium"; the G "must be the standard color version … and appear on a white background"; "You can download our pre-approved Sign in with Google buttons provided in PNG and SVG formats". https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines · retrieved 2026-09-29 · Amended 2026-09-30: the pre-approved files are in the archive https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip · retrieved 2026-09-30
