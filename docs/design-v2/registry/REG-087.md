### REG-087 · Is the app inside the European Accessibility Act?
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Art. 2(2) lists these services: electronic communications, access to audiovisual media, parts of passenger transport, consumer banking, e-books, and e-commerce.
  - A free, audio-only app with no purchases does not appear on the face of that list.
  - In-app purchases would raise "e-commerce services" ("…at the individual request of a consumer…").
  - "Microenterprises providing services shall be exempt…" (Art. 4(5)).
  - National transpositions may be wider (UNRETRIEVED).
