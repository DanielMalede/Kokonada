### CMP-032 · Honour Spotify Free playback restrictions
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The player reads `PlaybackRestrictions` and never offers an action Spotify restricts for Free users. The guidelines recommend offering play and pause only.
- source: Spotify Design Guidelines, "Playing views": "It is recommended that no play controls other than play/pause are provided in your app." "Use the restrictions returned in PlaybackRestrictions to correctly set the playback state in the companion app and to respond to the user when they try to perform a restricted action." https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P2 (the no-Premium state), P3
- surfaces: skip, previous and seek controls (MB-002, MB-042)
- verify at: G12 — a capture with a Free test account, which needs an allow-list slot
- severity if broken: P1
- depends on: CMP-033
