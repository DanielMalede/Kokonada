### CMP-132 · Reference boards and other brands' designs never ship
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: V0 and V1 inspiration material (screenshots of other apps, other brands' design systems) stays in private planning files. It never appears in app assets, the listing or public docs, and no shipped screen copies another brand's distinctive interface.
- source: Google Play Deceptive Behavior (impersonation). Spotify Developer Policy III.11. Google Play "Add preview assets" (third-party trademarks). · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Google Play, Spotify
- applies to: V0, V1, F4, P13
- surfaces: the board folders
- verify at: G5
- severity if broken: P1
