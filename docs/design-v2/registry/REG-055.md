### REG-055 · The right to object, where legitimate interests are used
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Where any processing rests on Art. 6(1)(f), the user can object. The right is "explicitly brought to the attention of the data subject and… presented clearly and separately from any other information", at the latest at the first communication.
