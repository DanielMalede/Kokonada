### REG-242 · Texas TDPSA: no sale of sensitive data without consent
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: Sensitive data is never sold without prior consent. Kokonada sells none today.
- source: Tex. Bus. & Com. Code §541.107: a small business "may not engage in the sale of personal data that is sensitive data without receiving prior consent". §541.002 excludes SBA small businesses from most duties.
