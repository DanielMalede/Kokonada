### REG-081 · No clinical or diagnostic state labels
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No state, band, domain, gauge or history label on screen uses a condition, symptom or diagnostic word: panic, anxious or anxiety, depressed or low mood, acute stress, burnout, disorder, symptom, exhausted, clinical. `FORBIDDEN_STATE_TERMS` (`stateDisplayVocabulary.ts:60-61`) stays a fail-closed guard.
