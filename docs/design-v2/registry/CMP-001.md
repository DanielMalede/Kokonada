### CMP-001 · Public surfaces never depend on Spotify playback
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No page state, flow or store asset presents Spotify playback as available to the public. Every page that carries playback works with no music provider, and the Spotify path is labelled as a beta for allow-listed testers.
- source: Spotify, "Quota modes": "Up to 5 authenticated Spotify users can use an app that is in development mode"; "The app owner must have a Spotify Premium account for apps in development mode to function"; for extended quota, "Spotify only accepts applications from organizations (not individuals)" and "at least 250k MAUs". https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/concepts/quota-modes. Also Spotify, "February 2026 migration guide" ("Users per app: 5"; "If the owner's Premium subscription lapses, the app will stop working"), https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/tutorials/february-2026-migration-guide · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P2, P3, P4, P6, P13, store listing
- surfaces: the provider-agnostic player view-model (P3); the Spotify states in P2; `mobile/KokonadaHealth/src/experience/connect/providers.ts:26-34` (Spotify is `halted`; keep it that way); listing copy and screenshots
- verify at: G1 — the brief carries both variants (Spotify beta and no provider). G12 — the no-provider variant is captured. Store submission — the listing promises no Spotify playback.
- severity if broken: P0
