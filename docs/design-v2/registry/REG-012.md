### REG-012 · Does the session flow "interact directly" with people?
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Counsel decides whether the session flow (wheel, free text, Live mode, playlist) is an AI system "intended to interact directly with natural persons". If it is, people must be told "that they are interacting with an AI system, unless this is obvious…".
