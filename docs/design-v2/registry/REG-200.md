### REG-200 · UK: explicit consent before any wearable health data
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Kokonada does not read, store or run inference on a UK user's heart rate, HRV, sleep or resting heart rate until that user has given explicit consent for specified purposes. No other Art. 9(2) condition is relied on unless counsel names one.
- source:
  - UK GDPR Art. 9(1) and 9(2)(a): "the data subject has given explicit consent to the processing of those personal data for one or more specified purposes". https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/article/9
  - Art. 4(15): "…which reveal information about his or her health status". https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/article/4
  - DPA 2018 s.10(2): the Schedule 1 Part 1 conditions unlock only Art. 9(2)(b), (h), (i) and (j). https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/section/10 (the fetcher paraphrased the subsections).
  - retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
- jurisdiction: UK
- classification assumption: heart rate, HRV and sleep data on the wearable lane, linked to an identified user, is data concerning health. This holds under every answer to the pivot.
- applies to: P5, P6, P7, backend ingest gates, privacy notice
- surfaces: the v3 consent document, the Connect wearable card, the order of the OS Health Connect sheet
- verify at: G5 (the consent copy names specified purposes) and G12 (a device capture shows no Health Connect read and no Bluetooth start before a current grant)
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: none
- status: NEEDS COUNSEL. Counsel should confirm that no Schedule 1 route is intended. If one is, DPA 2018 Sch. 1 paras 38–39 require an appropriate policy document.
