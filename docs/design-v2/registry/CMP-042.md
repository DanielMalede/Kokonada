### CMP-042 · No hidden or audio-only YouTube playback
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: YouTube audio never plays without its video, from a background player, or split from the video. Any YouTube playback uses an embedded player whose viewport is at least 200×200 px, with nothing drawn over it. It autoplays only when more than half of it is visible, and only one player on a screen may autoplay.
- source: YouTube API Developer Policies, prohibitions ("separate, isolate, or modify the audio or video components of any YouTube audiovisual content"; "create, include, or promote features that play content, including audio or video components, from a background player"). YouTube Required Minimum Functionality: "Embedded players must have a viewport that is at least 200px by 200px." "You must not display overlays, frames, or other visual elements in front of any part of a YouTube embedded player, including player controls." Also the autoplay rules. https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/required-minimum-functionality · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: YouTube
- applies to: P2, P3
- surfaces: no YouTube player exists in `src` today (compliant)
- verify at: G1 — no YouTube audio sink in any charter
- severity if broken: P0
