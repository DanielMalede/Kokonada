### REG-214 · UK: withdrawal as easy as giving consent
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: For UK users, withdrawing Art. 9 consent is as easy as giving it, and the user is told about this right before consenting. The shipped two-step withdrawal (`docs/SCREENS.md:162`) is to be measured against this rule.
- source:
  - UK GDPR Art. 7(3): "Prior to giving consent, the data subject shall be informed thereof. It shall be as easy to withdraw as to give consent".
  - Art. 7(1) (demonstrating consent) and Art. 7(2) ("clearly distinguishable from the other matters").
  - https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/article/7. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
- jurisdiction: UK
- classification assumption: consent is the Art. 9 condition (REG-200).
- applies to: P5, P9
- surfaces: the Withdraw control, the consent document's "your controls" section
- verify at: G5 and G12
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: REG-200
