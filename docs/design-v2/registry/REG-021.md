### REG-021 · High-risk conformity before placing on the market
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Under HR-Y, a system placed on the EU market on or after 2 Dec 2027 first completes the Chapter III provider duties:
  - risk management, data governance, technical documentation and logging;
  - instructions for use, human oversight, and accuracy and robustness;
  - quality management, conformity assessment and registration;
  - an EU authorised representative if the provider is outside the EU.
