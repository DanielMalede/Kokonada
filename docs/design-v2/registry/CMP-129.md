### CMP-129 · Kokonada's identity never resembles provider marks
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: The icon, splash, brand mark, tab glyphs and loaders avoid the YouTube play-button shape and red, Spotify's green, circle and waves (CMP-029), and Garmin's tag logo.
- source: Google Play Deceptive Behavior (apps cannot "falsely claim to be the official app of an established entity" or "attempt to mimic functionality or warnings from the operating system or other apps"). Spotify Design Guidelines (CMP-029). · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Google Play, Spotify, YouTube, Garmin
- applies to: P1, P12, P13, F1, V1
- surfaces: brand-mark tokens, `bootsplash`, the adaptive icon
- verify at: G5
