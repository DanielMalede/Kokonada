### CMP-028 · No implied Spotify endorsement or co-branding
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No copy says Spotify picks, recommends or endorses anything. The Spotify mark never shares a lockup with Kokonada's, Garmin's, YouTube's or Health Connect's mark; each source gets its own attribution slot.
- source: Spotify Developer Policy II.1 (no "endorsement, tie-in, co-branding or promotion by Spotify"). Spotify Design Guidelines: "Don't use the Spotify brand together with any other brand or in any co-branded communications. Pairing of brands is not permitted under our Developer Terms." https://developer.spotify.com/policy ; https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P3, P4, P13, F10
- surfaces: the Receipt stays visually separate from the mark (correct today); P4 Receipts that show several sources
- verify at: G5 — the asset and copy review
- severity if broken: P1
