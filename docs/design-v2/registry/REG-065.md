### REG-065 · Reading heart rate from the phone under Art. 5(3)
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Counsel decides whether reading Bluetooth heart rate and Health Connect records, and sending them to the server, is a "gaining of access" that needs Art. 5(3) consent on top of Art. 9, or falls under the exemption for a service the user explicitly requested. Counsel also decides whether the Art. 9 consent text can carry both.
  - As read on 2026-09-23, EDPB 2/2023 paras 53, 58 and 60 treat two things as a gaining of access: sending data produced on the device to a server, and a device passing data on over Bluetooth.
