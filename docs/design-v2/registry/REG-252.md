### REG-252 · Israel: section 11 notice at the point of collection
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Every request for personal data (sign-in and consent) carries a notice that states:
  - whether giving the data is a legal duty or depends on the user's consent;
  - the purpose;
  - the controller's name and contact details;
  - to whom the data is transferred and for what purposes;
  - the rights of access (s.13) and correction (s.14).
- source: PPL s.11 opens: "פניה לאדם לקבלת מידע אישי לשם עיבודו במאגר מידע תלווה בהודעה שיצויינו בה" [gloss: a request for personal data for processing in a database shall be accompanied by a notice stating…].
