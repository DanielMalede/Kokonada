### REG-208 · UK: no claims about restoring, correcting or modifying a physiological function
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No surface, notification, screenshot or listing claims or implies that Kokonada or its music restores, corrects or modifies a physiological function, or prevents or treats disease, unless the claim is true and evidenced. Examples:
  - "lowers your heart rate";
  - "improves HRV";
  - "calms your nervous system";
  - "improves sleep".
- source:
  - DMCC Act 2024 Sch. 20 para 19(1): "Falsely claiming that a product is able to—(a) prevent or treat disease or a malformation, (b) restore, correct or modify a physiological function, or (c) modify a person's appearance."
  - In force 6 April 2025 (S.I. 2025/272).
  - https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/13/schedule/20. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
- jurisdiction: UK
- classification assumption: holds on every branch. This UK text is wider than the EU UCPD Annex I point 17, which covers claims about curing illnesses.
- applies to: P2, P3, P4, P7, P13, store listing
- surfaces: the copy catalogue, the listing, screenshots
- verify at: G5 (copy freeze) and store submission
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: none
- status: SETTLED (as a copy rule)
