### CMP-111 · Predictive back
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: No code depends on `onBackPressed` or on intercepting `KEYCODE_BACK`. Every overlay (Genesis, Consent, Up Next, Receipt, disclosures) has a designed back outcome. Back on a root tab is not intercepted. Back on a consent or disclosure screen always means "no".
- source: Android 16 behaviour changes: for apps targeting 36 on Android 16, "the predictive back system animations … are enabled by default. Additionally, `onBackPressed` is not called and `KeyEvent.KEYCODE_BACK` is not dispatched anymore." Predictive back guide (updated 2026-09-18). https://developer.android.com/guide/navigation/custom-back/predictive-back-gesture · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Android
- applies to: F5, P1, P2, P3, P4, P5, P6
- surfaces: `UpNextSheet.tsx:85` (`BackHandler`, not verified on Android 16)
- verify at: G11 — a recording of back on every overlay
- severity if broken: P1
