### CMP-105 · (Later, iOS) Sign in with Apple button specification
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Use the system button: black on light backgrounds, white on dark. It is at least 140×30 pt, its margin is at least one tenth of its height, it is no smaller than the other sign-in buttons, and it never needs scrolling to find.
- source: Apple HIG "Sign in with Apple" (page updated 2022-09-14): "Make a Sign in with Apple button no smaller than other sign-in buttons, and avoid making people scroll to see the button"; minimum 140 pt × 30 pt; margin 1/10 of the height; the style rules. https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Apple
- applies to: P11 (iOS, later)
- surfaces: `SignInScreen.tsx:60` (the style choice is correct)
- verify at: G11 (iOS)
- severity if broken: P1
- depends on: none
- status: SETTLED
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
