### REG-089 · DSA: only the hosting limb is in question
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Art. 25 cannot attach to this app, for two reasons:
  - It binds "Providers of online platforms". An online platform needs dissemination "to a potentially unlimited number of third parties" (Art. 3(i) and (k)), and the app shares nothing publicly.
  - Art. 25(2) excludes "practices covered by Directive 2005/29/EC or Regulation (EU) 2016/679".
  - The only possible hook is "hosting" ("storage of information provided by, and at the request of, a recipient of the service", Art. 3(g)(iii)), for the stored free-text prompts and history.
  - If counsel reads the app as hosting, the hosting-service duties follow (those articles were not fetched in this run).
  - A public sharing feature would reopen Art. 25.
