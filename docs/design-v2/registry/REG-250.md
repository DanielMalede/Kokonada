### REG-250 · Israel: health data is "information of special sensitivity"; consent is informed and explicit
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: An Israeli user's wearable data is processed only with informed consent, given as an active and explicit act, never implied.
- source:
  - PPL s.3 lists "מידע אישי המתייחס למצב בריאותו של אדם, ובכלל זה מידע רפואי כהגדרתו בחוק זכויות החולה" [gloss: personal data relating to a person's state of health, including medical information as defined in the Patient's Rights Law]. The fetcher placed this under s.3, not s.7 as I first expected.
  - "הסכמה" – "הסכמה מדעת, במפורש או מכללא" [gloss: informed consent, express or implied].
  - Nevo, https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/71631.htm [REPRODUCTION]
  - The PPA's consent opinion is final as of March 2026 per commentary [COM, EBN]: "ברוב המקרים עדיף לפנות לקבלת הסכמה מפורשת" [gloss: in most cases explicit consent is preferable]. The PPA text is UNRETRIEVED (gov.il returned 403).
