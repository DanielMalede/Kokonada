### REG-232 · Washington: consumer health data privacy policy linked from the homepage
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: A consumer health data privacy policy exists and is linked prominently from the homepage and the app. It discloses:
  - the categories collected;
  - the purposes;
  - the sources;
  - the categories shared;
  - the third parties;
  - the user's rights.
  No new category or purpose is added without first disclosing it and obtaining affirmative consent.
