### REG-020 · Tell people they are subject to a high-risk system
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Under HR-Y, from 2 Dec 2027, deployers of Annex III systems "that make decisions or assist in making decisions related to natural persons shall inform the natural persons that they are subject to the use of the high-risk AI system". The v2 `AiDisclosure` keeps a second-sentence slot for this.
- source: AI Act Art. 26(11) [K1] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION; carries the Omnibus banner)
- jurisdiction: EU
- classification assumption: only if HR-Y, and only if choosing music is a decision "related to natural persons" (counsel's question)
- applies to: P2, P4, P7; Stage 0 F10
- surfaces: the second line of `AiDisclosure`
- verify at: G1 — the slot is reserved
- severity if broken: P1
