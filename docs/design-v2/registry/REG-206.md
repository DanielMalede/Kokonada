### REG-206 · UK: an exemption on record for each device store or read
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Every item Kokonada stores on, or reads from, a UK user's device has the Sch. A1 exception it relies on recorded. That covers:
  - the Keychain tokens;
  - the encrypted MMKV store;
  - the Health Connect reads;
  - the Bluetooth heart-rate reads.
  Nothing outside "strictly necessary" is added, including any analytics or crash SDK, unless the para 5 conditions or consent are met.
- source:
  - PECR reg. 6(1): "Subject to Schedule A1, a person must not store information, or gain access to information stored, in the terminal equipment of a subscriber or user". Reg. 6(2) extends this to "collecting or monitoring information automatically emitted by terminal equipment".
  - Sch. A1 para 4: "strictly necessary for the provision of an information society service requested by the subscriber or user".
  - Sch. A1 para 5 (statistics): "clear and comprehensive information about the purpose" and "a simple means of objecting, free of charge".
  - https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/regulation/6 and https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/schedule/A1 (revision date 5 Feb 2026). retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
- jurisdiction: UK
- classification assumption: holds on every branch. The facts: no analytics SDK (r_regulatory F12); the plan's default of no mobile analytics.
- applies to: P6, P1, backend, privacy notice
- surfaces: the Bluetooth lane, the Health Connect lane, the declared fallback report sent to the server if the shader fails (plan default)
- verify at: G1 and G12
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: none
- status: SETTLED
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
- note: reg. 6(2) "information automatically emitted" may also reach a non-user's broadcast heart rate (brief_regulatory N2). That is for counsel; see `MB-020`.
