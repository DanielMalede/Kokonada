### CMP-064 · Garmin's AI transparency statement in the privacy policy
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: If the Garmin RHQ variant applies, the privacy policy carries a conspicuous AI transparency statement, and the app gets explicit consent before an AI system processes Garmin user data, with easy withdrawal. Design P5 and the privacy policy to meet this either way.
- source: Agreement FRM-0952 Rev. B, RHQ variant §15.10: "Licensee's Privacy Policy shall include a conspicuous artificial intelligence transparency statement … if End User Data will be used for training with, or processing by, artificial intelligence systems … (i) obtaining explicit consent from each End User before initiating any processing of End User Data; (ii) allowing End Users to withdraw such consent easily at any time". The Garmin International variant has no such clause. · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Garmin
- applies to: P5, P14, store listing
- surfaces: the hosted privacy policy; the P5 consent copy (with REG- entries)
- verify at: store submission
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-051
- status: NEEDS OWNER
