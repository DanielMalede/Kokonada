### CMP-096 · Screenshot and graphic rules
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Screenshots show the real app interface only: no device frames, taglines covering at most 20% of the image, and no "Download now", rankings, time-limited claims, or third-party trademarks without permission.
- source: Google Play "Add preview assets": screenshots must "demonstrate the actual in-app or in-game experience"; taglines "should not take up more than 20% of the image"; no device frames; prohibited: rankings and "call-to-action" language like "Download now"; avoid "Third-party trademarked characters or logos without proper permission". https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151 · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Google Play
- applies to: P13, store listing
- surfaces: screenshots (no "N% read confidence", no "Readiness · Garmin-only", no real artists)
- verify at: store submission
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-037, CMP-086
- status: SETTLED
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
