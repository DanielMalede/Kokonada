### CMP-081 · Privacy policy link inside the app (Play)
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: A link to the hosted privacy policy is inside the app (sign-in, consent sheet, Vault, About) and in the Play Console field.
- source: Google Play "Health Content and Services": "Your app must post a privacy policy link in the designated field within Play Console, and a privacy policy link or text within the app itself." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16679511 · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Google Play
- applies to: P5, P9, P11, P14, F10 (LegalLinks), store listing
- surfaces: no link anywhere in `src` (MB-034); no hosted policy (DAN-015)
- verify at: G12. Store submission.
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: none
- status: HALT
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
