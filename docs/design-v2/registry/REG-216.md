### REG-216 · UK: a sharing feature triggers the Online Safety Act
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Before any feature lets content a user generates "be encountered by another user" (shared sessions, public playlists), the user-to-user regime is assessed. The product facts reviewed here contain no such feature.
- source: Online Safety Act 2023 s.3(1) defines a "user-to-user service" as a service through which content "generated directly on the service by a user … may be encountered by another user". https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/3. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
