### REG-261 · Israel: service accessibility, regulation 35
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Unless the reg. 35ו exemption applies, the app:
  - meets IS 5568 at level AA;
  - applies reg. 35ג to applications;
  - publishes an accessibility statement in a prominent place (reg. 35ה).
  Counsel says whether a free app falls inside the exemption.
- source: Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations 5773-2013, Nevo (amended 26 Oct 2017) [REPRODUCTION]. https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/500_865.htm. retrieved 2026-09-29. The relevant text:
  - reg. 35(א): "…ברמה AA";
  - reg. 35ג: "יישומים (אפליקציות)";
  - reg. 35ה: "חייב יציין במקום בולט … הצהרה";
  - reg. 35ו: "חייב שהוא עוסק פטור … או שהמחזור השנתי הממוצע שלו אינו עולה על 100,000", which is valid "למשך שלוש שנים" [for three years].
  - IS 5568 itself is UNRETRIEVED (a paid standard).
