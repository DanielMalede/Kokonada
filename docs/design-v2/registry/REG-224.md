### REG-224 · COPPA: the service is not directed to children
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Neither the app nor its listing is directed to children under 13. That means no child-oriented subject matter, visuals, characters or incentives, and no music framed to appeal to children. The Play target audience excludes children.
- source:
  - 16 CFR 312.2: a "Child" is "an individual under the age of 13".
  - The factors for "directed to children" include "subject matter, visual content, use of animated characters or child-oriented activities and incentives, music or other audio content, age of models…".
  - eCFR API, part 312 (90 FR 16977, 22 Apr 2025). https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-09-01/title-16.xml?part=312. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
