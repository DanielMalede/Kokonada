### REG-236 · Connecticut: sensitive data in scope regardless of volume
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Sensitive data of Connecticut consumers, including consumer health data, is processed only with consent. The CTDPA reaches any controller that processes such data, whatever its volume.
- source: CT Attorney General, CTDPA page. https://portal.ct.gov/ag/sections/privacy/the-connecticut-data-privacy-act. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED, guidance]. The page says:
  - the Act applies to a controller that "Controlled or processed consumers' sensitive data, excluding data processed solely to complete payment transactions";
  - "the CTDPA applies to all Consumer Health Data Controllers that do business in Connecticut, regardless of their size";
  - "a controller needs a consumer's consent to process sensitive data".
  - The statute (Conn. Gen. Stat. §42-515 and following, as amended by P.A. 25-113, effective 1 Jul 2026 per commentary) is UNRETRIEVED: cga.ct.gov failed TLS certificate verification.
