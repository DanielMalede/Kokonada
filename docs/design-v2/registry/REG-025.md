### REG-025 · An EU representative if the controller is outside the EU
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Under CTRL-3C, designate a representative in the Union in writing and name it in the notice.
  - The exemption from this duty needs processing that "does not include, on a large scale, processing of special categories of data".
- source: GDPR Art. 27(1), Art. 27(2)(a); Art. 13(1)(a) ("…and, where applicable, of the controller's representative") [K6] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION)
- jurisdiction: EU
- classification assumption: only if CTRL-3C
- applies to: P5, P11, P14; privacy notice
- surfaces: representative line
