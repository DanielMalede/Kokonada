### CMP-119 · OFL fonts stay under the OFL
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No bundled OFL font is sold on its own or offered under any licence other than the OFL, in docs, notices or packaging.
- source: OFL 1.1, condition 1 ("…may be sold by itself" is forbidden) and condition 5 ("must be distributed entirely under this license, and must not be distributed under any other license"). · retrieved 2026-09-29
