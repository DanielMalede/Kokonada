### CMP-139 · A disconnect made on Spotify's side deletes within 5 days
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: When a user removes Kokonada's access on Spotify's side (for example at spotify.com/account/apps), or Spotify or the user requests the disconnection, the app stops accessing that user's Spotify personal data and deletes it within 5 days. The trigger is `invalid_grant` only, never `invalid_client`, a 5xx or a network error; deletion lands within 5 days of the first `invalid_grant`, so the re-check falls on day 4 at the latest; a circuit breaker stops a mass purge; and a daily token-health refresh for linked users finds a revocation without waiting for the user to open the app. Split from `CMP-023` on 2026-09-30.
- source: Spotify Developer Terms V.8: "when a user disconnects their Spotify Service account or otherwise expresses an intent to prevent your SDA from accessing their data, you agree to delete and no longer request or process any of that user's Spotify Personal Data." Appendix A §5.c: "If a Data Subject disconnects accounts as described in (b) above, or if Spotify or a Data Subject requests such disconnection, you shall discontinue accessing the applicable Spotify Personal Data and delete the applicable Spotify Personal Data in your possession or control within five (5) days." Terms "Version 10, effective as of 15 May, 2025". https://developer.spotify.com/terms · retrieved 2026-09-30
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: backend, P9
- surfaces: `backend/app/services/spotify.js:126-130` turns a 400 or 401 from Spotify into `reconnect_required` and deletes nothing.
- verify at: CI — tests for the trigger, the day-4 re-check and the circuit breaker, including a burst of `invalid_client` errors that purges nothing. It must close before `SPOTIFY_BETA_CONNECT` is enabled for anyone else, and before any Spotify quota review.
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-023
- status: HALT
