### CMP-110 · Edge-to-edge: every screen handles insets
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Every screen applies the system-bar and cutout insets. Legal controls (Agree/Decline, AI notice, legal links) are fully visible between the status bar and a 3-button navigation bar at every font scale. This is required because apps targeting 36 cannot opt out on Android 16.
- source: Android 16 behaviour changes (updated 2026-09-16): "For apps targeting Android 16 (API level 36), `R.attr#windowOptOutEdgeToEdgeEnforcement` is deprecated and disabled, and your app can't opt-out of going edge-to-edge." https://developer.android.com/about/versions/16/behavior-changes-16. Android 15: "you must handle insets". https://developer.android.com/about/versions/15/behavior-changes-15 · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Android
- applies to: F5, P1–P14
- surfaces: `mobile/KokonadaHealth/android/gradle.properties:44` (`edgeToEdgeEnabled=false`, which is ignored); `ConsentSheet.tsx:256,268`; `SignInScreen.tsx:120`; Generate (MB-038, UI-023)
