### REG-215 · UK: transfers to US recipients
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: UK users' data goes to a US recipient only if the recipient is on the DPF List as participating in the UK Extension, or another UK transfer safeguard is in place. The notice names the destination and the safeguard. The possible US recipients are:
  - Railway;
  - MongoDB Atlas, if it is US-hosted;
  - the LLM inference sub-processor;
  - Sentry, if it is enabled.
- source:
  - S.I. 2023/1028 reg. 3(2): the recipient must be "indicated on the Data Privacy Framework List as participating in the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework". https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/1028/regulation/3/made [RETRIEVED]
  - ICO: "Partial adequacy: United States (US). Only covers personal information transferred under the UK Extension". Israel is on the full-adequacy list. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/adequacy-regulations/is-the-restricted-transfer-covered-by-adequacy-regulations/ [RETRIEVED]
  - Railway DPA: "Company's primary processing operations take place in the United States". UK transfers are made under the DPF or the UK Addendum; the DPA is executed through DocuSign. https://railway.com/legal/dpa [RETRIEVED; provider terms]
