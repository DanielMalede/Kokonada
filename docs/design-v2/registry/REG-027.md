### REG-027 · Consent is an express statement
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The Art. 9 grant is an affirmative act on a screen whose own words state consent to health-data processing for the named purposes. Scrolling, continuing or dismissing is not consent.
- source: GDPR Art. 4(11) ("…by a statement or by a clear affirmative action…") and Art. 9(2)(a) [K6] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION).
