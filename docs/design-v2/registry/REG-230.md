### REG-230 · Washington: consent before collecting consumer health data
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Consumer health data from Washington consumers is collected only with prior consent for a specified purpose, unless the collection is "necessary to provide a product or service that the consumer has requested". The consent request clearly and conspicuously states:
  - the categories of data collected;
  - the purpose, including the specific ways the data will be used;
  - the categories of entities it is shared with;
  - how to withdraw.
