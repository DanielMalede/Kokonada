### REG-258 · Israel: data received from the EEA (the 2023 Regulations)
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: If EU users' data sits in a database "in Israel", Kokonada:
  - deletes it on a written request;
  - runs a mechanism that removes data no longer needed;
  - runs a mechanism that keeps data accurate;
  - notifies data subjects as reg. 6 requires.
- source: Privacy Protection (Instructions for Data Transferred to Israel from the EEA) Regulations 5783-2023, Nevo [REPRODUCTION]. https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/215876.htm. retrieved 2026-09-29. The relevant text:
  - scope: "מידע המצוי במאגר מידע בישראל אשר הועבר מהאזור הכלכלי האירופי";
  - reg. 3: "ימחק מידע לבקשתו הכתובה של נושא המידע";
  - regs. 4 to 6 as above.
- jurisdiction: IL
- classification assumption: the controller is in Israel and EU users are served.
- applies to: P9, backend
- surfaces: Delete, export
- verify at: counsel
- severity if broken: P1
- depends on: REG-249
- status: NEEDS COUNSEL
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
