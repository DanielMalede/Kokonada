### CMP-011 · Cover art corner radius is 4 or 8
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Cover art uses corner radius 4 on phones and 8 on large devices (smallest width 600 dp and up). This is the `radius.art` token, and nothing else sets the art's radius.
