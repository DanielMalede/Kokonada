### REG-044 · Full Art. 13 information at sign-in
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Sign-in collects email, name and the SSO ID. "At the time when personal data are obtained", the user gets:
  - who the controller is, and its contact details;
  - the purposes and the legal basis;
  - the recipients and the transfer information;
  - retention, rights and withdrawal.
  - A short first layer with a link to the rest is a practice from guidance (WP260, UNRETRIEVED).
  - Today sign-in shows no notice and no link. A search of `mobile/KokonadaHealth/src/auth/SignInScreen.tsx` for `privacy|terms|policy` finds nothing.
