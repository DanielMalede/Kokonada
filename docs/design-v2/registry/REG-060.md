### REG-060 · Is a DPIA required before processing?
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Counsel decides whether the processing is "likely to result in a high risk", in particular as "processing on a large scale of special categories of data" (Art. 35(3)(b)). If so, the DPIA is done "prior to the processing", with the content Art. 35(7) requires.
  - The WP248 rev.01 criteria (sensitive data; evaluation or scoring, including health; innovative technology) are known here only from commentary.
  - The lead authority's Art. 35(4) list depends on REG-024.
