### CMP-010 · Spotify never plays without cover art and metadata
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: While Spotify content plays, its cover art and metadata are on screen. A placeholder may appear only briefly, while loading or retrying, and never as the resting state. Design boards use labelled cover-art slots, never gradients.
- source: Spotify Developer Policy II.5: "If you offer a Streaming SDA, metadata and cover art may only be used in connection with the underlying content, and there shall be no playback of Spotify Content without showing relevant cover art and metadata in your SDA." https://developer.spotify.com/policy · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P2 (the Genesis hand-off), P3, P1 (mini-player), F10 (CoverArt)
- surfaces: `NowPlayingScreen.tsx:160-178` (the "♪" placeholder); boards (TASKS UI-003)
- verify at: G12 — captures during playback, and during a slow art load and its retry
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-002
- status: SETTLED
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
