### REG-031 · Is mood-only input health data?
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Counsel decides whether these are "data concerning health" on the mood-only lane:
  - wheel taps;
  - free-text prompts (`PlaylistSession.contextPrompt`, stored encrypted);
  - inferred states.
  - If they are (MO-9), explicit consent comes before the first tap, and the emotion coordinates sent to the US LLM inference sub-processor (`docs/PRIVACY_DECLARATIONS.md:74`) are special-category data.
