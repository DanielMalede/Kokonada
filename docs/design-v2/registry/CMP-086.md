### CMP-086 · No misleading health or capability claims
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No screen, listing text or asset states a health claim that is misleading or clinical, or an accuracy or capability the product does not have. That includes coverage numbers presented as "read confidence", clinical or predictive state labels, "reads your mind", and live Garmin.
- source: Google Play "Health Content and Services": the policy prohibits "apps with health and medical related functionalities that are misleading or potentially harmful". Google Play Deceptive Behavior: apps "must not misrepresent their functionality, or claim impossible features". https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888077 · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Google Play
- applies to: P2, P4, P7, P8, P10, P13, store listing
- surfaces: `PulseScreen.tsx:207-210` prints "`N`% read confidence", which measures rule coverage (UI-008). State labels go through a closed display set (`stateDisplayVocabulary.ts`, merged). `stateTaxonomy.js` `DOMAINS` (including `stress`) and the History titles have no client allow-list yet (UI-020).
