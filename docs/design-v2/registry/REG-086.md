### REG-086 · As a Data Act third party: no dark patterns, no unneeded profiling
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: Under DA-Y, three rules apply:
  - do not make the user's choices "unduly difficult… by coercing, deceiving or manipulating the user… including by means of a user digital interface" (Art. 6(2)(a));
  - do not "use the data it receives for the profiling, unless it is necessary to provide the service requested by the user" (Art. 6(2)(b));
  - process the data only "for the purposes and under the conditions agreed with the user" (Art. 6(1)).
- source: Data Act Art. 6 [K14] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION). Recital 38 ("Third parties or data holders should not rely on so-called 'dark patterns' in designing their digital interfaces") [K14] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (RETRIEVED)
