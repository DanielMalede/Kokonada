### CMP-013 · Cover art is never animated or distorted
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: The art stays in its original form. No animation, parallax or gyro tilt, blur, tint, scale pulse or field overlay. Motion may move only what is behind the art.
- source: Spotify Design Guidelines: "Artwork must be kept in its original form"; "Don't animate or distort it in any way." https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: F3, F9, F10, P3, V1
- surfaces: the "album-art gyro-parallax" in `docs/UI_UX_OVERHAUL_SPEC.md` and `docs/SCREENS.md` must not carry into v2; `useMotionPolicy`
- verify at: G5 — the motion table lists the art as static. G11 — the filmstrip shows the art unchanged across frames.
