### REG-211 · UK: MHRA status (software as a medical device or wellbeing product)
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Counsel says whether any feature qualifies as SaMD before the P13 listing copy and the P7 vocabulary are frozen. The features in question are emotion inference, stress-adaptive pacing and the Pulse bands. Until counsel answers, no claim states diagnosis, prevention, monitoring, treatment or alleviation of disease or of a mental-health condition.
- source:
  - UK MDR 2002 reg. 2(1): "intended by the manufacturer to be used for human beings for the purpose of diagnosis, prevention, monitoring, treatment or alleviation of disease". "Intended purpose" is set "according to the data supplied by the manufacturer on the labelling, the instructions for use and/or the promotional materials". https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/618/regulation/2 [RETRIEVED; revision date 17 Jun 2025, with outstanding changes]
  - MHRA news, 27 Jan 2026: "Some digital mental health technologies are classed as medical devices, for example those claiming to diagnose, treat or manage a mental health condition… some are instead classed as wellbeing or lifestyle products". https://www.gov.uk/government/news/mhra-issues-new-guidance-for-people-using-mental-health-apps-and-technologies [RETRIEVED]
  - DMHT guidance (published 3 Feb 2025, updated 29 Jul 2026): the landing page is RETRIEVED; the PDF is UNRETRIEVED (see §9).
