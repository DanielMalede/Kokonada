### CMP-094 · Audience excludes children, and assets do not appeal to them
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The Play target audience excludes every group that includes children. Store graphics, animation style and characters do not suggest the app is for children. The owner sets the minimum age with counsel.
- source: Google Play "Manage target audience": "Any apps that have at least one target audience age group that includes children must comply with Google Play's Families Policy Requirements"; listings may be rejected if graphic assets, animation style or characters suggest child appeal. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159 · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Google Play
- applies to: P10 (age-step slot), P13, V0, V1, store listing
- surfaces: the "playful" feel; playful rounded type such as Fredoka in store art
- verify at: G5 — asset review. Store submission.
