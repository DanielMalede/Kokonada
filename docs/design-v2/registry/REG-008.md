### REG-008 · Notice clear, distinguishable, never buried
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The notice is "clear and distinguishable".
  - It is visible without a tap and is not collapsed.
  - It does not live only in the terms or the privacy notice.
  - It is never drawn on an inferred-state tint.
  - It stays up for as long as the surface shows inferred output. This last point is a build choice, not something the text requires.
- source: AI Act Art. 50(5) ("…in a clear and distinguishable manner…") [K1] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION). Art. 50 Guidelines point 142 [K4] (COMMENTARY: "Information is neither if it can easily be overlooked, for instance in a manual or in the terms of use")
