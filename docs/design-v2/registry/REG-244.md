### REG-244 · Illinois: no offering or advertising of therapy
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No copy offers, advertises or implies therapy or psychotherapy services. Examples: "therapeutic", "helps you process", "a regulator for your mind".
- source: Illinois Public Act 104-0054 (the Wellness and Oversight for Psychological Resources Act, 2025) — UNRETRIEVED (ilga.gov refused the connection). Commentary [COM] reports that an entity "may not provide, advertise, or otherwise offer therapy or psychotherapy services … unless … conducted by an individual who is a licensed professional". retrieved 2026-09-29
