### CMP-023 · Delete Spotify personal data within 5 days of disconnect
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: When a user disconnects Spotify, the app stops accessing that user's Spotify personal data and deletes it within 5 days, session history included. · Amended 2026-09-30: the app also provides a working, easily accessible mechanism to disconnect Spotify at any time, with clear instructions on how to do so (Terms V.8, first sentence; Appendix A §5.b; Developer Policy I.2). Today no mechanism exists. Revocation on Spotify's side is `CMP-139`.
- source: Spotify Developer Terms V.8: "when a user disconnects their Spotify Service account or otherwise expresses an intent to prevent your SDA from accessing their data, you agree to delete and no longer request or process any of that user's Spotify Personal Data." Appendix A §5.c: "…delete the applicable Spotify Personal Data in your possession or control within five (5) days." https://developer.spotify.com/terms · retrieved 2026-09-29 · Amended 2026-09-30: V.8, first sentence: "You must provide all users with a working and easily accessible mechanism to disconnect their Spotify Service account from your SDA at any time and provide clear instructions on how to do so." Appendix A §5.b: "You shall allow Data Subjects to disconnect their Spotify-provided account by providing an easily accessible and usable mechanism to disconnect their Spotify-provided account from your SDA at any time, including clear instructions on how to do so." Developer Policy I.2: "you must provide all users with an easily accessible mechanism to disconnect their Spotify account from your SDA at any time and provide clear instructions on how to do so." Terms "Version 10, effective as of 15 May, 2025" · retrieved 2026-09-30
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: backend, P9
- surfaces: the Spotify disconnect handler in `backend/app/controllers/integrationsController.js` never touches `PlaylistSession` (a grep finds no `PlaylistSession` in that file) · Amended 2026-09-30: `ProfileScreen.tsx:288-304` (the Spotify row, with no Disconnect); `integrationsController.js:189-212` (misses `PlaylistSession` and `spotifySessionPlaylistId`); `musicProfileService.js:675-689` (re-creates the profile by upsert); `spotify.js:126-130` (`CMP-139`).
- verify at: CI — a test runs disconnect and then the job, and finds zero rows derived from Spotify
