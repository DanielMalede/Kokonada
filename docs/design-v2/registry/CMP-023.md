### CMP-023 · Delete Spotify personal data within 5 days of disconnect
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: When a user disconnects Spotify, the app stops accessing that user's Spotify personal data and deletes it within 5 days, session history included.
- source: Spotify Developer Terms V.8: "when a user disconnects their Spotify Service account or otherwise expresses an intent to prevent your SDA from accessing their data, you agree to delete and no longer request or process any of that user's Spotify Personal Data." Appendix A §5.c: "…delete the applicable Spotify Personal Data in your possession or control within five (5) days." https://developer.spotify.com/terms · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: backend, P9
- surfaces: the Spotify disconnect handler in `backend/app/controllers/integrationsController.js` never touches `PlaylistSession` (a grep finds no `PlaylistSession` in that file)
- verify at: CI — a test runs disconnect and then the job, and finds zero rows derived from Spotify
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-022
- status: HALT
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
