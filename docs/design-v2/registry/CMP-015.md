### CMP-015 · No sound over Spotify audio
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No sound of any kind overlaps or mixes with Spotify playback: no earcons, UI sounds, crossfades, ambient beds or voice. "Music fades in" means starting playback, never a Kokonada audio layer.
- source: Spotify Developer Policy III.7: "Do not permit any device or system to segue, mix, re-mix, or overlap any Spotify Content with any other audio content (including other Spotify Content)." https://developer.spotify.com/policy · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P1, P2, P3, F3, F10
- surfaces: the optional earcon on track change in `docs/UI_UX_OVERHAUL_SPEC.md` (must not carry forward); Genesis
- verify at: CI — a guard fails if `src/design/v2` gains an audio asset or a sound API. G5 — the motion and sound table has no audio rows.
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: none
- status: SETTLED
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
