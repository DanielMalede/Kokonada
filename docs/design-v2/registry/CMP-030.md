### CMP-030 · No clone of Spotify's core experience
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: Now Playing and Up Next must not copy or replace Spotify's own player. Each carries Kokonada's own value (the Receipt, the field, re-tuning).
- source: Spotify Developer Policy III.11: "Do not build products or services that mimic, or replicate or attempt to replace a core user experience of Spotify or its group companies without our prior written permission. Your product or service must add independent value or functionality that improves users' interactions with Spotify." https://developer.spotify.com/policy · retrieved 2026-09-29
