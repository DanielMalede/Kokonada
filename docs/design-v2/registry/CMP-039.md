### CMP-039 · "YouTube" is never part of the app's name
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: The app's name, icon text and listing title contain no "YouTube" and no variant of it. "Works with YouTube" is allowed in descriptive copy.
- source: YouTube Branding Guidelines: "You must never use the YouTube name or any abbreviation, acronym, or variant of the word YouTube, such as YT or You-Tube in conjunction with the overall name of your application"; "you may reference the fact that your app is for YouTube or works with YouTube". https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/branding-guidelines · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: YouTube
- applies to: P12, P13, store listing
- surfaces: the listing title, the icon
- verify at: store submission
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: none
- status: SETTLED
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
