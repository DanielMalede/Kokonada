### REG-014 · No workplace or education deployment or marketing
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: The product is not sold, licensed, configured or marketed for use by employers or education institutions. No listing, site or in-app copy targets teams, employees, students or schools.
  - The prohibited practice: "…the use of AI systems to infer emotions of a natural person in the areas of workplace and education institutions, except where … for medical or safety reasons".
- source: AI Act Art. 5(1)(f), which applies since 2 Feb 2025 (Art. 113(a)). Recital 44 ("That prohibition should not cover AI systems placed on the market strictly for medical or safety reasons…") [K1] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION)
- jurisdiction: EU
- classification assumption: every option in which the system infers emotions. Whether Art. 5(1)(f), which is worded without the Art. 3(39) biometric condition, reaches inference from non-biometric input is counsel's question.
- applies to: P13, P14; store listing; any B2B channel
- surfaces: listing text, screenshots, About copy
- verify at: store submission — the listing is checked against this rule
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: REG-001
