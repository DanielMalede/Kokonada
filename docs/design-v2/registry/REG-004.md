### REG-004 · Who is the deployer that owes the notice?
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Counsel confirms whether Kokonada, which runs the inference on its own servers for consumers, is the "deployer" that Art. 50(3) addresses. A consumer is not a deployer when using the system "in the course of a personal non-professional activity".
