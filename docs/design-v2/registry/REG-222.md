### REG-222 · HBNR: breach-notice readiness
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: If Kokonada is a vendor of personal health records, a breach runbook exists before launch. It provides for:
  - notice to individuals "without unreasonable delay and in no case later than 60 calendar days after the discovery";
  - notice to the FTC. The rule's text says breaches of fewer than 500 individuals may go into an annual log (§318.5(c)); the timing for 500 or more is not quoted here;
  - notice content covering items (a) to (e) of §318.6;
  - an in-app messaging path, because "Electronic mail" is defined as "Email in combination with one or more of the following: text message, within-application messaging, or electronic banner".
- source: 16 CFR 318.4(a), 318.5(a)(1), 318.5(c), 318.6 and 318.2 ("Electronic mail"). eCFR API as in REG-221. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
