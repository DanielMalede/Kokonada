### CMP-097 · Store icon specification
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The store icon is a 512×512, 32-bit PNG (with alpha) of at most 1024 KB. It meets Play's icon design specification and carries no badge or text that suggests a ranking, a price or a Play category.
