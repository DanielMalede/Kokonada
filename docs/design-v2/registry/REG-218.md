### REG-218 · FTC: no health-benefit claim without scientific evidence
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No health-benefit claim appears anywhere unless it is backed by competent and reliable scientific evidence. Examples of such claims: stress reduction, a relaxation effect on the body, better sleep, higher HRV, improved mood. A disclaimer is never used to rescue a claim that lacks evidence.
- source: FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance (Dec 2022). https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]. It says:
  - health benefits need "competent and reliable scientific evidence", and "Randomized, controlled human clinical trials (RCTs) are … generally the type of substantiation that experts would require";
  - its scope includes "health-related apps";
  - "The DSHEA disclaimer or similar statements won't cure an otherwise deceptive ad".
