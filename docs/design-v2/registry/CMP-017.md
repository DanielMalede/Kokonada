### CMP-017 · Never show analysis derived from Spotify content
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No screen or asset shows BPM, energy, valence, audio features or any listening metric derived from Spotify tracks or the Spotify service.
- source: Spotify Developer Policy III.13: "Do not analyze the Spotify Content or the Spotify Service for any purpose, including without limitation, creating new or derived listenership metrics, benchmarking, functionality, usage statistics, user metrics, or building profiles of users, including for the purpose of targeting them with advertising or marketing." https://developer.spotify.com/policy · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P3, P4, P7, P8, P13, server plumbing
