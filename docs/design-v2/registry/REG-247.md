### REG-247 · Colorado SB 26-189: no inferred state in a consequential decision
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: No inferred state ever feeds a decision about access to, eligibility for, or compensation related to education, employment, housing, financial or lending services, insurance, health-care services, or essential government services and public benefits. Music selection is not on that list.
- source: Colorado SB 26-189 (signed 14 May 2026). The bill summary defines a "consequential decision" as one "that relates to an individual's access to, eligibility for, or compensation related to education, employment, housing, financial or lending services, insurance, health-care services, or essential government services and public benefits", and says the bill "repeals and reenacts" SB 24-205. https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/sb26-189. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED, summary]. SB 24-205 is [SUPERSEDED by SB 26-189].

- classification assumption: a trigger gate.
