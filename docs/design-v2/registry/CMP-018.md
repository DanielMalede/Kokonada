### CMP-018 · Personalising from Spotify listening data
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: It is unclear whether Kokonada's taste model counts as "building profiles of users" under Policy III.13. The model uses Spotify top artists and genres, and after plan B3 it will also use `track_skipped` and `playback_event` for Spotify tracks. Until the owner rules, Spotify-derived taste stays internal: never displayed, never shared, never used for marketing.
