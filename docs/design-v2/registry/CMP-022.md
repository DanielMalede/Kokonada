### CMP-022 · Spotify content is never stored indefinitely
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: Spotify content (track IDs, keys, titles and artists) is stored only as far as strictly necessary, and older data is deleted on a stated clock. It is never kept indefinitely. · Amended 2026-09-30: this entry covers `PlaylistSession` and `ServeEvent`; the `MusicProfile` part is `CMP-138`.
- source: Spotify Developer Terms IV.3.1.a: "you may not store, aggregate or create compilations or databases of Spotify Content, other than as strictly necessary to operate your SDA. You must use reasonable efforts to ensure that any data you display to users is the most up to date data available through the Spotify Platform, and to delete older data. Do not store Spotify Content indefinitely." https://developer.spotify.com/terms · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: backend, server plumbing, P8, P3
- surfaces: `backend/app/models/PlaylistSession.js:39-53` (`trackIds`, `trackKeys`, `trackSummary`; no TTL); `backend/app/workers/sessionTrim.worker.js:8,25` keeps `trackSummary` on purpose (TASKS BE-021); the planned B3 `playback_event` · Amended 2026-09-30: `ServeEvent.js:26` (90 days; Spotify ISRCs and titles in `canonicalKey`); `PlaylistSession.externalPlaylistId`; `sessionTrim.worker.js:17-23` (the filter only matches rows with sensitive fields). The three global corpora are Spotify-free by construction and counted by `spotifyLeakMonitor`.
- verify at: CI — a TTL or erasure test for every collection that holds Spotify IDs
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-024
- status: HALT
