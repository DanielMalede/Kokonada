### CMP-037 · No real artists, tracks or cover art outside live playback
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: Onboarding, design boards, Design Lab fixtures, marketing and store screenshots use made-up track and artist names and made-up art. Real Spotify content appears only in live playback, with attribution.
- source: Spotify Developer Policy II.3: "Don't confuse or mislead anyone as to the artist, creator or user associated with any Spotify Content." Google Play, "Add preview assets": graphics must avoid "Third-party trademarked characters or logos without proper permission". https://developer.spotify.com/policy ; https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151 · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify, Google Play
- applies to: V0, V1, F4, P10, P13, store listing
- surfaces: "Holocene" and "Bon Iver" on the boards (UI-003)
- verify at: G5 — the asset review. Store submission — the screenshot review.
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: none
- status: SETTLED
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
