### CMP-093 · Organization account for a health app
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: The owner decides whether Kokonada is a "health app" that must be published from a verified organization account with a D-U-N-S number. Garmin (CMP-051) and Spotify's extended quota (CMP-001) also need a business entity.
- source: Google Play "Choose a developer account type": organization accounts are required for "Health apps, such as Medical apps and Human Subjects Research apps"; "To create a developer account for an organization, you must have a D-U-N-S number." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885. Play Console Requirements ("effective September 30, 2026"; risk of "global removal from Google Play"). https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10788890 · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Google Play
- applies to: store listing
- surfaces: the Play account
