### CMP-093 · Organization account for a health app
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Kokonada is published on Google Play only from a verified organization developer account with a D-U-N-S number. No personal Play developer account is created for it, and no build goes to any Play track, internal testing included, from a personal account. This holds whatever the medical-device answer is: Kokonada reads health data, so it must declare health features, and Play's policy links "Health apps" to categories that include "Health and fitness apps". A medical answer makes the rule certain. Only a written statement from Google Play that an app declaring only Health and fitness features is outside Play Console Requirements 1.2 would reopen this row. · Amended 2026-10-04: "effective September 30, 2026" and "global removal from Google Play" were wrongly attributed to this rule. They belong to Play package-name registration (answer/16984799) and Android developer verification. This rule has bound new accounts since the policy announcement of 17 July 2024.
- source: Play Console Requirements: "When creating your Play Console account, developers providing the following services must register as an Organization:" … "1.2. Health apps, such as Medical apps and Human Subjects Research apps. Learn more about Health app categories." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10788890. Health app categories: "Health apps encompass a wide range of applications that are designed to support and improve users' health, well-being (mental and physical), and medical management"; the types are "Health and fitness apps", "Medical apps" and "Human subjects research apps". https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13996367. Health apps declaration: "If your app provides health features or accesses health data to support non-health features, you must declare one or more of the following features". https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14738291. D-U-N-S: "This process can take up to 30 days so you should plan ahead." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312. Origin: "We're requiring developers providing the following services to register as an Organization: financial products and services, health, VPN, and government." "This update will roll out to new developer accounts first." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14993590 · retrieved 2026-10-04
