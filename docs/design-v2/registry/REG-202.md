### REG-202 · UK: whether a UK representative is required
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: If the controller is not established in the UK and Art. 3(2) applies, a UK representative is designated in writing and named in the notice. The exemption for occasional processing does not cover "large scale" processing of special-category data.
- source:
  - UK GDPR Art. 27(1): "Where Article 3(2) applies, the controller or the processor shall designate in writing a representative in the United Kingdom".
  - Art. 27(2)(a): the exemption requires processing that "does not include, on a large scale, processing of special categories of data".
  - https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/article/27. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
- jurisdiction: UK
- classification assumption: the controller is outside the UK (see DEC-044).
- applies to: P11, P14, privacy notice
- surfaces: the representative line in the sign-in first layer and in the notice
