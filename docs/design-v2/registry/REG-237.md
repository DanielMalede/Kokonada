### REG-237 · California CCPA: whether Kokonada is a "business"
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: The owner tracks the CCPA thresholds. If Kokonada becomes a "business", sensitive personal information about health that is used to infer characteristics becomes subject to the right to limit (§1798.121).
- source: Cal. Civ. Code §1798.140(d)(1). https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1798.140. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED; last amended by AB 1170, effective 1 Jan 2026]. It covers:
  - "annual gross revenues in excess of twenty-five million dollars";
  - "annually buys, sells, or shares the personal information of 100,000 or more consumers or households";
  - "50 percent or more of its annual revenues from selling or sharing".
  - §1798.140(ae) lists "Personal information collected and analyzed concerning a consumer's health".
  - §1798.121(d) exempts sensitive personal information "collected or processed without the purpose of inferring characteristics about a consumer".
