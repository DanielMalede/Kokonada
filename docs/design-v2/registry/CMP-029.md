### CMP-029 · App identity never resembles Spotify
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: Kokonada's icon, splash, brand mark, name and tab glyphs use no Spotify Green and no circle-with-waves shape, and nothing that looks like them. The name contains no "Spot"-like start and no "Spotify".
- source: Spotify Design Guidelines: "Don't include, or look similar to the Spotify logo or any of its brand elements (e.g. Spotify Green, the circle, and the waves)." "The app name should not include 'Spotify' or be similar to 'Spotify' in sound or spelling." Spotify Developer Policy VI: "the name should not begin with 'Spot' or be confusing in sound or spelling to Spotify." · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P12, P1 (tab glyphs), F1, V1, P13
- surfaces: `bootsplash` assets, the adaptive icon, the brand-mark tokens
- verify at: G5 — the brand-mark and icon review
- severity if broken: P1
