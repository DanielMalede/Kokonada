### CMP-107 · (Later, iOS) Health data rules
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: Health data is never used or disclosed for advertising, marketing or data mining, and the app discloses the specific health data it collects.
- source: App Store Review Guidelines 5.1.3(i): "Apps may not use or disclose to third parties data gathered in the health, fitness, and medical research context … for advertising, marketing, or other use-based data mining purposes…"; "You must disclose the specific health data that you are collecting from the device." · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Apple
- applies to: P5, P13 (iOS, later)
- surfaces: consent copy; App Privacy answers · Amended 2026-10-02: the App Privacy purposes, once the learning lane is live (`QA-132`): Health and Fitness, and Usage Data's Product Interaction ("music listening data"), add Product Personalization ("Customizing what the user sees, such as a list of recommended products, posts, or suggestions"). And Track B, the global `TrackPosterior`, learns from a reward that is 60% heart-rate slope (`backend/app/agents/runtime/learning/feedbackLoop.js:299-308,371-373`): if HealthKit-sourced samples reach the play window, health-derived outcomes are pooled across users to rank music for others, which is plausibly "other use-based data mining" under 5.1.3(i) (the auditor's interpretation, UNVERIFIED). The compliant alternative feeds Track B only the behavioural component. An iOS gate, P2.
- verify at: store submission (App Store)
- severity if broken: P0
