### REG-009 · Notice in an accessible format
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The notice is built for assistive technology and large text.
  - It is a single accessibility node, reached by TalkBack before the controls that follow it.
  - It reaches at least 4.5:1 contrast against the worst field frame, in both themes.
  - It is not clipped at 200 % font size.
- source: AI Act Art. 50(5) ("The information shall conform to the applicable accessibility requirements.").
