### REG-010 · An emotion recognition deployer must also comply with GDPR
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The deployer "shall process the personal data in accordance with Regulations (EU) 2016/679…". A GDPR failure on the inference lane, such as processing heart rate without a current grant (BE-029), is then also an Art. 50(3) failure.
- source: AI Act Art. 50(3) [K1] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION)
