### CMP-012 · Cover art is never cropped or covered
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: Cover art is never cropped, and nothing is placed on it: no text, images, playback controls or logos.
- source: Spotify Design Guidelines: "Don't crop the artwork in any way." "Don't overlay images or text on top of the artwork." "Don't cover the artwork with playback controls." "Don't place your brand or logo on top of album artwork." https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: F10 (CoverArt), P3, P4
- surfaces: `NowPlayingScreen.tsx:164` uses `resizeMode="cover"`, which crops art that is not square
- verify at: G11 — a capture with non-square art shows it whole
- severity if broken: P1
- depends on: CMP-011
- status: SETTLED
