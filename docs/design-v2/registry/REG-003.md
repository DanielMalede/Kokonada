### REG-003 · Are AI Act duties live for today's testers?
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Counsel decides which of these describes serving the owner plus up to five hand-enrolled testers. Today that is the owner alone (`TASKS.md` DAN-021, update 2026-09-29).
  - "Putting into service": "the supply of an AI system for first use directly to the deployer or for own use in the Union for its intended purpose" (Art. 3(11)). This is PS-Y.
  - Excluded testing: "research, testing or development activity regarding AI systems or AI models prior to their being placed on the market or put into service" (Art. 2(8)). This is PS-N.
