### REG-239 · California CMIA: provider of health care, or mental health digital service?
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: No copy "markets itself as facilitating mental health services". Counsel says whether Kokonada is a business deemed a provider of health care under §56.06(b) or (d).
- source:
  - Cal. Civ. Code §56.06(b) covers a business offering a "mobile application … designed to maintain medical information".
  - §56.05(l) defines a mental health digital service as an app "that collects mental health application information from a consumer, markets itself as facilitating mental health services to a consumer, and uses the information to facilitate mental health services".
  - §56.05(k) defines mental health application information as "information related to a consumer's inferred or diagnosed mental health or substance use disorder".
  - https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=56.05 (amended by SB 81, effective 20 Sep 2025) and https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=56.06. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
- jurisdiction: US-CA
- classification assumption: VISION §1 positions the app as a place "to process what they're going through". That line must not reach any copy a user sees.
- applies to: P2, P13, P14
- surfaces: the listing, onboarding
- verify at: counsel and G5
- severity if broken: P1
