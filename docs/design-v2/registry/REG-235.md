### REG-235 · Nevada SB 370: consent to collect and to share
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Nevada consumers' health data is collected and shared only with affirmative consent, obtained separately for each, unless an exception applies. There is no size threshold.
- source: NRS 603A.400–603A.550 — UNRETRIEVED: leg.state.nv.us and law.justia.com returned HTTP 403 on 2026-09-29. What is known comes only from commentary [COM], which reports a definition based on use ("uses to identify the past, present or future health status"), an in-force date of 31 Mar 2024, and enforcement by the Attorney General only.
