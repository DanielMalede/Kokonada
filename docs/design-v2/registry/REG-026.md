### REG-026 · Explicit consent before any processing of wearable health data
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Kokonada reads, sends, stores, infers from or shows heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate or sleep **only** while a current explicit Art. 9 grant exists. That holds at every step:
  - ingest;
  - the serving path;
  - `GET /api/pulse/state` (BE-023);
  - the workers (BE-029).
- source: GDPR Art. 9(1) and 9(2)(a) ("…explicit consent to the processing of those personal data for one or more specified purposes…") [K6] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION). Recital 35 ("…any information on… the physiological or biomedical state of the data subject independent of its source, for example from… a medical device…") [K5] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (RETRIEVED). C-21/23 para 83 ("…capable of revealing information about the health status of the data subject by means of an intellectual operation involving collation or deduction") [K17] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION)
- jurisdiction: EU
- classification assumption: required by law if live heart rate is data concerning health (Track H's open question H-A). The owner built the gate for every option (PR #193), so it stands as a build requirement.
- applies to: P2, P5, P6, P7; backend
- surfaces: `mobile/KokonadaHealth/src/health/healthLane.ts`, `/watch/token`, `request_heart_playlist`, the Pulse read, the workers
- verify at: G1 — the gate map. G12 — on a fresh mood-only install there is no OS prompt and no `/watch/token` call.
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: none
- status: SETTLED
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
