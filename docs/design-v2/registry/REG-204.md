### REG-204 · UK: assess and document likely access by children
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Before a UK launch, the controller records a documented assessment of whether under-18s form "a substantive and identifiable user group". The result selects one of two paths: conform to the Children's code standards, or use age assurance to keep under-18s out.
- source:
  - ICO, "Likely to be accessed by children": "If the nature, content or presentation of your service makes you think that children will want to use it, then you should conform to the standards in this code".
  - The same page: "If you decide that your service is not likely to be accessed by children...you should document and support your reasons for your decision".
  - https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/likely-to-be-accessed-by-children/. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED, guidance; no date shown on the page]
- jurisdiction: UK
- classification assumption: a general-audience music and emotion app with no age gate.
- applies to: P10, P13 (target audience), Stage 0 L1
- surfaces: the age slot, the Play target-audience setting
- verify at: counsel and store submission
- severity if broken: P1
- depends on: DEC-050
- status: NEEDS OWNER (the owner writes the assessment and counsel reviews it)
