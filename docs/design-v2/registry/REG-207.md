### REG-207 · UK: complaints channel with a 30-day acknowledgment
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: UK users can make a data-protection complaint to the controller through an electronic channel that can be reached from P9 and P14, and every complaint is acknowledged within 30 days.
- source:
  - DPA 2018 s.164A, inserted by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 s.103. It opens: "A data subject may make a complaint to the controller if the data subject considers…". Its duties to facilitate electronic complaint forms and to acknowledge within 30 days are summarised here in the fetcher's paraphrase.
  - In force 19 June 2026 under S.I. 2026/82 reg. 3(a).
  - https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/18/section/103. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED; the full subsection text should be read verbatim]
- jurisdiction: UK
- classification assumption: UK GDPR applies because UK users are targeted.
- applies to: P9, P14, privacy notice
- surfaces: a "Privacy complaint" entry, the contact route
