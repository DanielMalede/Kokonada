### REG-070 · No claims about health effects
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No copy, listing, screenshot, notification or caption says the app or its music reduces stress or anxiety, lowers heart rate, improves HRV or sleep, calms, regulates or treats. Copy describes the music and how it was chosen.
- source: UCPD Art. 6(1)(b) ("…the results to be expected from its use…") and Annex I point 17 ("Falsely claiming that a product is able to cure illnesses, dysfunction or malformations.") [K13] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION); MDR Art. 2(12) [K11] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION)
- jurisdiction: EU
- classification assumption: every option
- applies to: P2, P3, P4, P7, P10, P12, P13, P14; store listing
- surfaces: every copy key
- verify at: store submission, plus G5 (copy lint)
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: none
- status: SETTLED
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
