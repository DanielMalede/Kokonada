### REG-219 · FTC: no accuracy claim beyond the evidence; foreseeable harms assessed
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: No claim about the accuracy of the inferred state or of the readings goes beyond the evidence. Before launch, the foreseeable harms of the inference are assessed and recorded, such as misreading distress and bias.
- source: FTC press release of 18 May 2023 on the Policy Statement on Biometric Information. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/05/ftc-warns-about-misuses-biometric-information-harm-consumers [RETRIEVED]. It says:
  - "Biometric information refers to data that depict or describe physical, biological, or behavioral traits, characteristics, or measurements of or relating to an identified or identifiable person's body";
  - the factors the FTC will consider include "Failing to assess foreseeable harms to consumers before collecting biometric information".
  - The policy statement PDF itself is UNRETRIEVED.
  - retrieved 2026-09-29
- jurisdiction: US-federal
- classification assumption: heart rate and HRV fall within "measurements … relating to … a person's body". That reading is for counsel.
