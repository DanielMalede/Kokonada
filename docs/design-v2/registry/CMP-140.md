### CMP-140 · Health data is encrypted at rest and in transit on every host that holds it
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Data the app reads through Android health permissions is encrypted at rest and in transit wherever Kokonada stores or moves it, Railway's Redis and private network included, and the Health apps declaration says so on each host's own published statement, not on a forum post.
- source: Google Play "Android Health Permissions": "…at minimum, data encryption both at rest and in transit…". https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134 · retrieved 2026-10-02
- provider: Google Play, Health Connect
- applies to: backend, store listing
- surfaces: Railway's encryption of Redis at rest and of its private network in transit rests on a staff forum post, which is not a contract: "All data in all volumes are encrypted as rest." (2025-07-15, https://station.railway.com/questions/what-security-claims-can-i-make-about-po-448e15db). The owner confirms both at trust.railway.com, and the Railway sub-processor row records it before the Health apps declaration (TASKS `DAN-049`, item 3). The auditor rated the finding LOW.
- verify at: store submission — the Railway sub-processor row and the Health apps declaration cite Railway's own statement for both, with its date
