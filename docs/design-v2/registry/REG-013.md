### REG-013 · Machine-readable marking of LLM-written captions
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: If the LLM-written discovery captions ship, each caption carries a machine-readable mark that makes it "detectable as artificially generated or manipulated". The exception is where the system performs "an assistive function for standard editing".
  - The captions sit behind the `DISCOVERY_CAPTION_LLM` flag (`backend/app/services/discovery/captionService.js:5-20`) and are attached at `backend/app/sockets/biometricHandler.js:171-173`.
