### CMP-021 · The YouTube library is played through Spotify
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: The backend takes the user's YouTube liked videos, finds matching tracks through Spotify search and plays them on Spotify. Spotify forbids products "integrated with streams or content from another service". This path must not run until the owner has Spotify's position, or drops it.
- source: Spotify Developer Policy III.5: "Do not create any product or service which is integrated with streams or content from another service." https://developer.spotify.com/policy · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: backend, P2, P3, P6
- surfaces: `backend/app/services/crossPlatform.js:57-95` (`translateToSpotify`, `translatedFrom:'youtube'`); `backend/app/services/musicProfileService.js:568-641` (YouTube and Spotify taste merged)
- verify at: CI — a test shows the translation is off unless the flag is on, and the flag is pinned off. G1 — the P3 brief.
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-046
- status: HALT
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
