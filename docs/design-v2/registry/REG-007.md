### REG-007 · First exposure: start of processing or first output?
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: For a wearable user, counsel decides which moment is "first … exposure":
  - the start of processing after the Art. 9 grant on Connect (a recompute is queued 60 s after the first sync, `backend/app/services/wearable/metricStore.js:188-195`), or
  - the first rendered output, on Generate.
  - If it is the first, the notice also goes on the Connect wearable card (P6).
