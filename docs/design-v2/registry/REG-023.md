### REG-023 · AI literacy measures
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The provider and deployer takes measures on the AI literacy of its staff and of anyone operating the systems on its behalf, and records those measures.
- source: AI Act Art. 4, pre-Omnibus text ("…shall take measures to ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level of AI literacy of their staff and other persons dealing with the operation and use of AI systems on their behalf…") [K1] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION, SUPERSEDED).
