### CMP-113 · Bluetooth permissions match the lane that is kept
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` (with `neverForLocation`) and `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT` are declared only while the Bluetooth lane exists. The legacy permissions stay capped at `maxSdkVersion="30"`, with the Location prompt explained. All of them are removed if the lane becomes Health Connect only.
- source: Android Bluetooth permissions (updated 2026-09-16), covering the runtime "Nearby devices" permissions, `neverForLocation` and the legacy `maxSdkVersion="30"` entries. https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/bluetooth/bt-permissions. Google Play "Permissions…": only permissions "necessary to implement current features". · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Android, Google Play
- applies to: P6, Q1
- surfaces: `AndroidManifest.xml:22-27` declares them, but nothing in production calls the request (MB-053)
