### CMP-071 · Screens for too little access and for two cancels
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Every Health Connect entry point shows a designed "insufficient access" state. After two cancels in a row, the app shows a screen that sends the user to Health Connect settings and never prompts again by itself.
