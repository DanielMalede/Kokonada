### CMP-100 · Google button padding and label
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: On Android the padding is 12 before the G, 10 after it and 12 after the text. The label is "Sign in with Google", "Sign up with Google" or "Continue with Google" (translation is allowed). "Google" alone is never the label, and the G never appears without the button boundary and text.
- source: Sign in with Google branding guidelines: "Android & Web … 12px left padding before the Google logo, 10px right padding after the Google logo and 12px right padding after the Sign in with Google text"; label options; "Don't use 'Google' by itself to represent the action". · retrieved 2026-09-29
