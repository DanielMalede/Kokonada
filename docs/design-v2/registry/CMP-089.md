- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The Data safety answers list all data collected or shared, including by SDKs (Google Sign-In, Spotify App Remote, Health Connect, Bluetooth) and backend processors, and include the planned server failure report. - source: Google Play Data safety: "You alone are responsible for making complete and accurate declarations in your app's store listing on Google Play"; declarations cover data handled "through third-party libraries and SDKs". https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469 · retrieved 2026-09-29
