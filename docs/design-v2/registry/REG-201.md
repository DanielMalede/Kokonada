### REG-201 · UK: first-layer notice at sign-in
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: P11 shows a first layer before the sign-in completes account creation. It gives:
  - the controller's identity and contact details;
  - the UK representative, if REG-202 requires one;
  - the purposes and the legal basis;
  - the recipient categories;
  - a link to the full notice. The full notice carries the Art. 13(1)(f) transfer information.
- source:
  - UK GDPR Art. 13(1)(a): "the identity and the contact details of the controller and, where applicable, of the controller's representative".
  - Art. 13(1)(c), (e) and (f).
  - https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/article/13 (revision date 5 Feb 2026). retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
- jurisdiction: UK
- classification assumption: holds on every branch. Sign-in collects the email address, the name and the SSO id (`docs/PRIVACY_DECLARATIONS.md:40-42`).
- applies to: P11, privacy notice
- surfaces: `SignInScreen.tsx`, which today has no privacy, terms or policy link (r_regulatory F4; `MB-034`)
- verify at: G5 (text against Art. 13(1)) and G12 (the link resolves on the device)
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: REG-202 and DEC-044
- status: NEEDS OWNER (the controller's identity)
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
