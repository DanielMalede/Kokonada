### REG-255 · Israel: registration, and notice to the PPA above 100,000 people
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The owner monitors the number of users. If information of special sensitivity is held on more than 100,000 people, the PPA is notified. Registration of the database applies only in the cases s.8(ג) lists.
- source:
  - PPL s.8א(ב): "עלה מספר בני האדם שיש על אודותיהם מידע בעל רגישות מיוחדת...על 100,000" [gloss: where the number of people exceeds 100,000].
  - s.8(ג) registration cases include a database whose "מטרתו העיקרית היא איסוף מידע אישי...לשם מסירתו לאחר כדרך עיסוק או בתמורה" [gloss: main purpose is collecting personal data to pass to others as a business or for consideration].
