### CMP-138 · MusicProfile's Spotify content has a refresh clock
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Spotify content held in `MusicProfile` (the `library` array and `knownArtistIds`) is refreshed or deleted on a stated clock, including while the user stays connected. It is never kept indefinitely. Split from `CMP-022` on 2026-09-30.
- source: Spotify Developer Terms IV.3.1.a: "Except as otherwise set out in these Developer Terms, you may not store, aggregate or create compilations or databases of Spotify Content, other than as strictly necessary to operate your SDA. You must use reasonable efforts to ensure that any data you display to users is the most up to date data available through the Spotify Platform, and to delete older data. Do not store Spotify Content indefinitely." "Spotify Content" includes "metadata, playlists, and user data including Spotify Personal Data". Terms "Version 10, effective as of 15 May, 2025". https://developer.spotify.com/terms · retrieved 2026-09-30
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: backend, P3
- surfaces: `backend/app/models/MusicProfile.js:36-66` (`library`: Spotify IDs, URIs, names, ISRCs, artist IDs and popularity) and `:28-30` (`knownArtistIds`: Spotify artist IDs). For a Spotify-only profile nothing refreshes or deletes them while the user stays connected: `backend/app/workers/youtubeRetention.worker.js` only selects profiles that contain `youtube_music` rows.
- verify at: CI — integration tests. Production — a count of 0 Spotify-bearing profiles with `lastAnalyzed` older than 30 days (DEC-101). It closes after HX-3, since both touch `musicProfileService.js`.
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-022
- status: HALT
