### CMP-104 · (Later, iOS) An equivalent login beside Google
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: On iOS, wherever Google sign-in is offered, Sign in with Apple is offered as an equal option. The Android layout keeps a reserved slot for it.
- source: App Store Review Guidelines 4.8: "Apps that use a third-party or social login service … must also offer as an equivalent option another login service" that limits collection to name and email, lets the email stay private, and does not track for ads without consent. https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Apple
- applies to: P11 (iOS, later)
- surfaces: `SignInScreen.tsx:54-60` (the system `AppleButton`); the entitlement is missing (DAN-011)
- verify at: store submission (App Store)
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-105
- status: SETTLED
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
