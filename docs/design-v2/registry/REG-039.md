### REG-039 · Before consent: the right to withdraw, and what stays
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: The consent layer states:
  - that consent can be withdrawn at any time;
  - that processing before the withdrawal stays lawful;
  - where to withdraw;
  - what withdrawal erases and what survives (session heart rate for up to 30 days, BE-016; the taste profile, ADR-0015).
