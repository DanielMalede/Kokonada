### CMP-027 · Never targeted at children
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: The app, its listing and its assets are not aimed at children. When the app knows a user is a child, it blocks the Spotify features for that user.
- source: Spotify Developer Policy III.8: "Do not build products and services which are targeted to children. If you have actual or suspected knowledge that specific people using your SDA or online service are children, you must block the use of the Spotify Platform for those people." https://developer.spotify.com/policy · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P10 (the reserved age-step slot), P13, store listing
- surfaces: the Play target-audience settings; the style of the onboarding and store art
- verify at: store submission — the target-audience answers
- severity if broken: P0
