### CMP-008 · Link back to the exact Spotify content
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Every Spotify metadata or cover-art item links to its own track, album or playlist on Spotify, for example `spotify:track:<id>` or `https://open.spotify.com/track/<id>`. It never links to the bare app. Each Up Next, Receipt or History item gets its own link.
- source: Spotify Developer Policy II.4.2: "Metadata, cover art and Audio Preview Clips must be accompanied by a link back to the applicable album, content or playlist on the Spotify Service." https://developer.spotify.com/policy · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P3, P4, P8, F10
- surfaces: `mobile/KokonadaHealth/src/experience/player/spotifyLinkBack.ts:27` calls `Linking.openURL('spotify:')`, the bare app. This is a breach.
- verify at: CI — a unit test checks the opened URI equals the item's URI. G12 — on the device, a tap lands on that track.
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-002
- status: HALT
- re-verify by: 2026-11-28
