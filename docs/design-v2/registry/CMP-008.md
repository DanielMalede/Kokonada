### CMP-008 · Link back to the exact Spotify content
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Every Spotify metadata or cover-art item links to its own track, album or playlist on Spotify, for example `spotify:track:<id>` or `https://open.spotify.com/track/<id>`. It never links to the bare app. Each Up Next, Receipt or History item gets its own link. · Amended 2026-09-30: when Spotify is not installed, the link opens the item's own page, `https://open.spotify.com/<type>/<id>`. · Amended 2026-11-25: DEC-193, which supersedes DEC-112, replaces the amendment of 2026-09-30: when Spotify is not installed, GET SPOTIFY FREE opens Spotify's app-store listing (Android `market://details?id=com.spotify.music`, then `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spotify.music`; iOS `https://apps.apple.com/app/spotify-music-and-podcasts/id324684580`; no parameters), and the title and the cover art each link to the item's own page, `https://open.spotify.com/<type>/<id>`, in either install state.
- source: Spotify Developer Policy II.4.2: "Metadata, cover art and Audio Preview Clips must be accompanied by a link back to the applicable album, content or playlist on the Spotify Service." https://developer.spotify.com/policy · retrieved 2026-09-29 · Amended 2026-09-30: Spotify Design Guidelines, "Linking to Spotify": "If you use any Spotify metadata (including artist, album and track names, album artwork and audio playback) it must always link back to the Spotify Service." "Always link to the Spotify app (when the Spotify client is available on platform)." "If the Spotify app is installed then use any of the following text strings: OPEN SPOTIFY, PLAY ON SPOTIFY or LISTEN ON SPOTIFY." "If the Spotify app is not installed then the link should say, GET SPOTIFY FREE." https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design · retrieved 2026-09-30. The Developer Policy is "Effective as of 15 May, 2025", retrieved 2026-09-30. · Amended 2026-11-25: Spotify Design Guidelines, "Linking to Spotify": "If the app is not installed, the user should be directed to the app store so that they can install Spotify." https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design · quoted by the compliance-auditor in hx4 round 2 (2026-09-30; TASKS DAN-041); CA307-15 read the live guidelines on 2026-11-25.
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P3, P4, P8, F10
- surfaces: `mobile/KokonadaHealth/src/experience/player/spotifyLinkBack.ts:27` calls `Linking.openURL('spotify:')`, the bare app. This is a breach. · Amended 2026-09-30: `UpNextSheet.tsx:182-184` and `UpNextRow.tsx:65-73`; `spotifyLinkBack.ts:9,40-46` (the not-installed branch opens the download page); `coverArtResolver.ts:32-42` (the foreign cover). · Amended 2026-11-25: on #311, the not-installed branch opens the store listing (`spotifyLinkBack.ts:73-84`, `openSpotifyStoreListing`), and Now Playing's title and cover open the item's page (`NowPlayingScreen.tsx:124-127`, through `openSpotifyWebPage`).
- verify at: CI — a unit test checks the opened URI equals the item's URI. G12 — on the device, a tap lands on that track. · Amended 2026-11-25: G12, under DEC-193: with Spotify not installed, a tap on GET SPOTIFY FREE lands on Spotify's store listing, and a tap on the title or the cover lands on that item's page (TASKS MB-201).
