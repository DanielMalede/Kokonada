### REG-005 · Tell exposed people the system is operating
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Every person exposed to the inference is told that an emotion recognition system is operating. The required content is the fact of "the operation of the system". It does not have to state the system's purpose.
- source: AI Act Art. 50(3) ("Deployers of an emotion recognition system or a biometric categorisation system shall inform the natural persons exposed thereto of the operation of the system…") [K1] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION). Commission FAQ ("This does not entail providing information regarding e.g. the purpose of the system's operation.") [K3] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (RETRIEVED)
