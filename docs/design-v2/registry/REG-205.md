### REG-205 · UK: DPIA before launch
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: A DPIA exists and is signed off before UK users' data is processed at launch. It covers:
  - the inference of emotional state;
  - the matching of data from several sources (Health Connect, Bluetooth, self-report);
  - the health data itself.
- source: The ICO's list of processing that requires a DPIA includes "processing involving the use of innovative technologies, or the novel application of existing technologies (including AI)" and "combining, comparing or matching personal data obtained from multiple sources". https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/data-protection-impact-assessments-dpias/when-do-we-need-to-do-a-dpia/. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
