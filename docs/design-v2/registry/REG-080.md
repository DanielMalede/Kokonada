### REG-080 · Does any feature create a medical purpose?
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: Counsel decides whether any feature or claim gives the product a medical intended purpose: the readiness score, the state labels, drift detection, or "senses distress… help regulate it".
  - Under MD-Y, the MDR applies: classification under Rule 11 and conformity assessment.
  - The AI Act then reaches the product through Annex I, from 2 Aug 2028.
