### REG-212 · UK: anticipatory reasonable adjustments
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Services to UK users are usable by disabled people generally. That includes consent, the notices, the AI disclosure and the rights controls. The working standard is WCAG 2.2 AA, which the project has already committed to (`docs/SCREENS.md:32,197`).
- source:
  - Equality Act 2010 s.29(1): "provision of a service to the public or a section of the public (for payment or not)".
  - s.29(7)(a): "A duty to make reasonable adjustments applies to—(a) a service-provider".
  - Sch. 2 para 2(2) applies the duty "to disabled persons generally".
  - https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/section/29 and https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/schedule/2. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
- jurisdiction: UK
