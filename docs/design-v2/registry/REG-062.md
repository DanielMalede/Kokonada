### REG-062 · A transfer mechanism for each recipient outside the EU
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: Each transfer outside the EU/EEA rests on an adequacy decision or on Art. 46 safeguards, and the notice says which.
  - For a US recipient, that means its Data Privacy Framework certification under Decision (EU) 2023/1795, or standard contractual clauses.
  - The regions of the hosting, the database and Redis, and whether Sentry is used, cannot be established from source (DAN-018).
- source: GDPR Art. 46(1), 46(2)(c) and 13(1)(f) [K6] · retrieved 2026-09-29 (REPRODUCTION). On the Data Privacy Framework: the General Court dismissed T-553/23 on 3 Sep 2025, and the appeal C-703/25 P is pending (COMMENTARY; CURIA UNRETRIEVED).
