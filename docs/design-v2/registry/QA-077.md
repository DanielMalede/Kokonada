#### Pass rule, restated 2026-10-04 (resilience-auditor)

The Design Lab is absent, not merely unreachable, from every build that can be uploaded to Play Console (any track, internal app sharing included) or App Store Connect (TestFlight included). Lab code is: any module under `src/design/lab/`, `index.lab.js`, anything under `android/app/src/lab/`, and any bundle holding `LAB_SENTINEL`. It passes on the commit under review when all of the following hold.

**A. On every PR, in CI, with no path filter**
1. The release entry is `index.js` on both platforms:
   - `index.android.js` and `index.ios.js` do not exist;
   - Gradle refuses to configure while `ENTRY_FILE` is set;
   - the iOS bundle phase refuses `ENTRY_FILE`, after sourcing `.xcode.env` and `.xcode.env.local`.
2. The import closure of `index.js`, across every platform suffix, holds no lab module and reaches `src/navigation/AppFlow.tsx`. No file outside `src/design/lab/`, `index.lab.js` and `scripts/lab/` has an import edge into the lab.
3. A Metro bundle of `index.js`, built with the release task's flags, together with its sourcemap and its Hermes bytecode, holds 0 sentinels and 0 lab sources, for Android and for iOS.
4. Positive control: the same pipeline over `index.lab.js` shows the sentinel in the JS and in the bytecode, and lab sources in the sourcemap. Otherwise the check fails.

**B. In CI, whenever `android/**`, `modules/**` or `scripts/lab/**` changes**
5. Manifests:
   - every merged manifest of every non-lab variant declares no lab component and is not the lab package;
   - the lab manifest is the lab package, with `DesignLabActivity` as its only launcher;
   - it has no BROWSABLE filter and none of the real app's components or schemes;
   - cleartext is off, and its permissions are within the allow-list.
6. The release classes hold no lab class, and the lab classes do (positive control).

**C. In every Gradle build, whatever the route**
7. Every non-lab asset merge fails if its output holds the sentinel. This covers hand-placed `src/main/assets` bundles.
8. Every lab App Bundle task is refused, flavors included.

**D. Before every upload**
9. The exact AAB, APK or IPA is scanned with `node scripts/lab/assertLabExcluded.mjs --scan`, and exit 0 is required. With no path, the scan exits 2. It also exits 2 unless it read at least one JS bundle and, on Android, at least one dex. The output and the artifact's sha256 go into the upload record.

The lab APK has no BROWSABLE intent filter. It is entered only by an explicit intent to `com.kokonadahealth.lab/.DesignLabActivity`, it is debug-signed, and it is not debuggable.

A and B close on green CI logs from the PR, not local runs. C closes on a planted RED/GREEN Gradle run. D closes on the first upload's evidence record.

Lab-variant evidence (`buildType: lab`) never satisfies a release-evidence clause (QA-031).

Clause A1's iOS part closes with the store-gate PR; until then clause D9's IPA scan is the iOS control.
