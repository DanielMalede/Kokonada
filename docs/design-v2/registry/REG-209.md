### REG-209 · UK: no misleading claims about accuracy or confidence
- kind: PROHIBITION
- rule: No claim about the accuracy of the inferred state goes beyond what the engine measures. Examples of claims that would breach this:
  - "100% read confidence", which is printed from rule coverage (`UI-008`; brief_regulatory N6);
  - "reads your mind";
  - "knows exactly how you feel".
- source:
  - DMCC Act 2024 s.226(1)(a): "the provision of false or misleading information relating to a product".
  - s.226(2): information "which, although true, is presented in a misleading way".
  - In force 6 April 2025.
  - https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/13/section/226. retrieved 2026-09-29 [RETRIEVED]
