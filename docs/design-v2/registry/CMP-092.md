### CMP-092 · Play's 12-tester closed test against Spotify's 5-user cap

- rule: A new personal Play account needs at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days before production, but only 5 accounts can use Spotify. The owner picks the route: an organization account, testers using the no-provider path, or another option.
