### CMP-092 · Play's 12-tester closed test against Spotify's 5-user cap
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: A new personal Play account needs at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days before production, but only 5 accounts can use Spotify. The owner picks the route: an organization account, testers using the no-provider path, or another option. · Amended 2026-10-04: with an organization account (CMP-093), the 12-tester / 14-day rule does not reach the account on its own terms. The page is titled 'App testing requirements for new personal developer accounts'. It does not say that organization accounts are exempt. Spotify's 5-user cap still limits who can test Spotify features.
- source: Google Play testing requirements (personal accounts created after November 13, 2023: "Minimum of 12 testers", "opted in continuously for at least 14 days"). https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465. Spotify "Quota modes" (as in CMP-001). · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Google Play, Spotify
- applies to: store listing, P13, P3
- surfaces: the Play account type
- verify at: store submission
- severity if broken: P0
- depends on: CMP-001, CMP-093
- status: SETTLED · Re-issued 2026-10-04 by `compliance-auditor`: `CMP-093` rules out a personal account for any Play track. The 12-tester / 14-day rule is limited to "personal developer accounts created after November 13, 2023" (answer/14151465), so it never reaches Kokonada's account, and no route is left for the owner to pick. Spotify's 5-user cap stays with `CMP-001`. This entry reopens only if `CMP-093` reopens (Google's written statement, `DAN-057`) and the owner then chooses a personal account.
