### CMP-033 · Is App Remote playback a "Streaming SDA"?
- kind: OPEN QUESTION
- rule: The owner decides whether Kokonada, which drives the Spotify app through App Remote, counts as a Streaming SDA. If it does, the Spotify path is for Premium users only and carries no commercial use of any kind. That already fits the 100% free decision.
- source: Spotify Developer Policy IV.1: "Streaming of music sound recordings through the Spotify Platform shall only be made available to subscribers to the Premium Spotify Service." IV.2 lists commercial uses barred for Streaming SDAs ("any e-commerce (e.g., in-app payment or monetization) initiated via a Streaming SDA; the sale of advertising, sponsorships, or promotions on the Streaming SDA itself"). https://developer.spotify.com/policy · retrieved 2026-09-29
- provider: Spotify
- applies to: P2, P3, P13
- surfaces: the no-Premium state in P2
- verify at: G1 — the P2 and P3 briefs
