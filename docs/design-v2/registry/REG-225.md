### REG-225 · COPPA: actual knowledge of a user under 13
- kind: OBLIGATION
- rule: If the age slot or an OS or store signal tells Kokonada that a user is under 13, it collects nothing further from that user, and deletes what it holds, unless verifiable parental consent is obtained.
