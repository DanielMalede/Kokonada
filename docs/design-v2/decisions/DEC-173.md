### The bottom line, Fork 2 · lines 12–13

> - **Fork 2:** use **(A) force sRGB at one choke point, plus a strengthened detect-and-record**. "Strengthened" means the screencap header check that already exists, plus an on-screen colour comparison patch (a "witness") that proves the runtime-effect path itself. Reject converting inside the shader. Defer Skia-managed wide gamut to a future ADR.
> - The code changes what the handoff says about Fork 2. On Android at 2.6.9 the on-screen Skia surface has no colour space, so nothing converts the shader output today. The real risks are iOS, a library upgrade, the app opting a window into wide colour, and how the compositor reads the buffer. The header check alone cannot see that last one.

### The recommendation · line 186

> **Recommendation: (A) plus a strengthened (C).** Force sRGB, and prove the path on the device with an on-screen comparison patch (a "witness"), not only with the header. Reject (B). Defer (D) to a future ADR, which must also re-specify the never-red byte-level analysis and the schema's gamut rule. **Flag for the designer:** this closes off P3 "bioluminescence" until that ADR.

### What B4's FieldView must do now · lines 188–195

> ### What B4's FieldView must do now
> 1. Render through one sRGB choke point: `<Canvas colorSpace="srgb">`, with `opaque` absent. Setting `opaque` switches to a SurfaceView, which is a different composition path and needs the witness re-run.
> 2. Keep the colour path unmanaged by construction:
>    - SkSL with no `layout(color)`, `toLinearSrgb` or `fromLinearSrgb`, and the manual `encode` as the output contract (`fieldShader.ts:88-90,173-174`);
>    - `setDither(false)`;
>    - in `effect.ts`, the blue-noise image created once, untagged (`MakeImage`), sampled nearest with no mipmaps.
> 3. Take every frame input as an explicit prop: state, plate geometry in px, the tier, and `renderScale` (passed explicitly or resolved by one pure function). The lab records them, so B8 can rebuild the noise-free expected colour with `fieldOklab` with grain and dither set to 0. No internal `Dimensions` reads and no hidden tier logic.
> 4. Record what B8 needs to sample around: renderScale and the upsampling filter. This also covers resilience's MEDIUM-2 fringe check, already on B4's list.

### Ruling 3a, Fork 2 (colour space) · lines 218–224

> **3a, Fork 2 (colour space). ACCEPT, with text added.** Take the architect's DEC as written, plus:
>
> > "…and no window opts into wide colour or HDR/extended-range headroom. This applies to every Skia surface the field draws through: the on-screen Canvas, and any offscreen surface or picture used for renderScale, which is sRGB or untagged and never P3. The palette is sRGB by design. Restrained chroma (anchors at C ≤ 0.11), the never-red margins and every contrast proof are defined on sRGB bytes. 'Bioluminescence' in this language means luminance and depth on a dark ground, not gamut ('luminous accents used as depth, not neon chaos', UI_UX_OVERHAUL_SPEC:59). A future wide-gamut ADR must come with a palette I author and re-proved never-red and contrast sets. Changing the pipeline alone is not allowed."
>
> - Nothing visible is lost.
> - It also removes the iOS mismatch between a misread field and correctly managed beads, which would have been a brand defect.
> - Ruling 1's ⌈σ⌉ rule is defined on exactly this contract.

### Ruling 3c, the witness scene · lines 234–249

> **3c, the witness scene. ACCEPT, with amendments.**
> 1. **Split W1.**
>    - W1a: the runtime effect with every ground set to the anchor and grain and dither at 0 (the construction the current Skia test uses). W1a, W2 and W3 then share one expected value, the token hex, and must agree within ±1.
>    - W1b: a production high-k patch. Compare the median of per-pixel (render − the law's noise-free colour) within ±1. The median is preserved under monotone encoding, so noise does not bias it.
> 2. **Colours, per face.**
>    - The four anchors at the face's tier. Joy and intense are mandatory, since they flank the red band. D5's discrimination rule applies to these.
>    - Plus the face's `ground`, as a seam check. It is the largest surface, and it must not jump on the first frame or at the swap to the fallback.
> 3. **Add a third planted control:** skip the encode, so linear bytes are read as sRGB.
> 4. **Patch layout.**
>    - Patches are at least 24×24 dp, 8 dp apart, sampled over their central 16×16 dp, on the field's ground.
>    - All 3 directions × 2 faces.
>    - The manifest records renderScale and the upsampling filter. The filter is Linear with no mipmaps (V0 parity); nearest would make the grain blocky.
> 5. **Production rule.** The ground is drawn only by the field, which carries the grain. No React Native View paints `color.<face>.ground` over the field canvas, and the canvas spans the full window, including under the system bars.
> 6. **Byte evidence runs in Natural mode only** (the harness rule). My eyes-on review on the device adds one pass in Vivid, Samsung's default. That pass is recorded as such and never used as byte evidence.
>
> A witness discrepancy is a STOP for my ruling.
