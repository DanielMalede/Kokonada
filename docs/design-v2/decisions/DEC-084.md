## The table ruled

`design-tokens/base.tokens.json` `field.tiers`, as it stands at `0763519`, is the ruling:

| tier | renderScale | octaves | fpsCap | shimmer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| full | 0.5 | 3 | 120 | 1 |
| low | 0.33 | 2 | 60 | 0 |
| still | — | — | 0 | — |

| trigger | tier |
|---|---|
| reducedMotion | still |
| powerSave | low |
| thermalSevere | still |

The scribe's notes:
- the table names one thermal status only, severe. It maps no status below severe to a tier; that is not ruled here.
- the full tier draws at the display's rate up to 120; on a 60 Hz display both moving tiers draw every frame (`UI-171`).

## What follows from it, as the designer ruled (not the owner's ruling)

The frame cap that enforces `fpsCap` (`UI-171`, `capFrame` in `mobile/KokonadaHealth/src/design/v2/field/fieldDriver.ts`) brought two rulings by the `designer`, after the `resilience-auditor`'s T14c and T14b audit at `199b138` (APPROVE, findings RA-39 and RA-40). No file of the designer's holds them; they are recorded on TASKS and in the code, and quoted from there.

### RA-39 · only a taps or layout change forces a draw past the cap (TASKS `UI-229`)

TASKS `UI-229`, its update of 2026-11-15:

> the forced draw follows a counter only a taps change or a layout change by value bumps (the `designer` ruled that layout must also draw at once); stress, confidence, genesis and quiet presence wait for the cap; `generation` stays the RA-22 guard.

FieldView's contract (`mobile/KokonadaHealth/src/design/v2/field/FieldView.tsx`, the frame cap):

> The first frame of the mount, after a tier change, after a taps or a layout change, or after a wake from sleep is always drawn; any other prop change is drawn on the next frame the cap allows (RA-39).

### RA-40 · the ledger's bounded lag behind the field clock (TASKS `UI-230`)

TASKS `UI-230`, its update of 2026-11-15: "the `designer` accepted the bounded lag at `199b138` and rewrote the sentence (the lag adds up over stalls and is never observable); it is in `frameDt.ts` and FieldView's contract word for word". The sentence, from `mobile/KokonadaHealth/src/design/v2/clock/frameDt.ts`:

> At a capped tier, each stall over motion.dtClamp that directly follows a skipped frame may leave the placement ledger up to one display frame behind the field clock. The lag adds up over stalls but is never observable, because nothing reads ledger time against clock time: it only lengthens a crossfade in progress by that much, and a settled ledger is unaffected.
