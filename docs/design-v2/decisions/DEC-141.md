### V1_AMENDMENT_COPYCHECK.md · §2, DSN-051 · lines 164–184

**DSN-051: the Living background is perceptible, disclosed and switchable** (REG-016's G12 test; owner Round 9 Q2).
- **The switch.**
  - The You page (P9) carries a switch labelled "Living background" / "רקע חי".
  - It is the user's own setting, stored per account, On by default.
  - The app never changes it and never suggests a change.
  - Nothing inferred ever sets it or prompts it: not tension, not confidence, not any reading.
- **Off means no field anywhere.**
  - The field RuntimeEffect is not run on any page.
  - Pages draw their ground and the plate's own materials through the static renderer, with lobe weight 0 and no halos.
  - Gone: lobes, halos, breath, drift, shimmer, the Genesis presence change, and every inferred-state response.
  - Unchanged: the CTA tint, the beads and the haptics.
- **Off composes with reduced motion.**
  - Reduced motion alone keeps a still field coloured by the user's placements. That still updates by 150 ms crossfades.
  - Off removes the field entirely.
  - Each is honoured on its own; both together equal Off.
- **The disclosure.**
  - It sits beside the switch.
  - It says what the field does on screen: its colours come from the user's points, it slows and dims when the readings run high, and turning it off changes nothing else.
  - It never says what the field does to the body. No calm, relax, regulate, breathe, stress, heart or health words (DSN-048, REG-070, REG-082, REG-084).
  - The copy is counsel's (copy check Q3). §3c C5's candidate is the starting draft.
- **When it ships.** The switch and the disclosure ship with P9. Until then the V1 lab exposes the same state as scene param `fieldEnabled`, captured as scene `fieldOff`.
