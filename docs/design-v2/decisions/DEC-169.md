# DEC-169 · A quiet zone is every point within `quiet.grow` of its measured bound: the rectangle with rounded corners (sdBox − grow), one formula in the law and the shader, at most six zones (V1 B4)

- status: DECIDED
- opened: 2026-10-06

- ruling: recorded 2026-11-17; the orchestrator's ruling under DEC-142, the designer's ruling and the agents' agreement are quoted below. The scribe's English gloss: a quiet zone (DSN-023) is the measured bound's rectangle grown by `quiet.grow` with its corners rounded to radius `quiet.grow`, not a square-cornered box. The law and the SkSL evaluate one formula at the pixel centre in render-target pixels: q = abs(p − c_j) − h_j; sd_j = length(max(q, 0)) + min(max(q.x, q.y), 0) − g; the factor is smoothstep(0, F, sd_j) when F > 0, else a step (1 where sd_j > 0); extent = regionExtent · the product of the factors over j < count. On and inside the grown boundary extent is exactly 0. Unused slots are inert through `count` only; a zero-size rectangle is not empty, it carves a disc of radius g. At most six zones: more is a RangeError in tests and the lab, and the shipped worklet clamps to six (the orchestrator's ruling on the architect's point 4, to which the designer had no objection).

## The designer's ruling, verbatim

### v1b-B4-designer-rulings-r3-corners-clip-qa068.md · the short answer and §1 · lines 5, 8 and 14–28 (handed back 2026-10-06T07:56:51Z)

> The three rulings I handed back earlier stand. These answer the architect's sequencing note (`v1b-B4-architect-sequencing.md` §5, points 1–3). Point 2 also corrects a gap in my own fallback spec: Linen's fallback clip never kept the field out of the text zones.

> - **Corners:** rounded (sdBox − grow).

> ## 1. Grown quiet-zone corners: ROUNDED (sdBox − grow)
>
> > **DEC (quiet-zone geometry).** A quiet zone is every point within `quiet.grow` of the measured bound. That is the bound's rectangle with its corners rounded to radius `quiet.grow` (sdBox − grow), not a square-cornered box. The law and the SkSL share one formula, evaluated at the pixel centre in field render-target pixels:
> > - q = abs(p − c_j) − h_j
> > - sd_j = length(max(q, 0)) + min(max(q.x, q.y), 0) − g
> > - factor_j = F > 0 ? smoothstep(0, F, sd_j) : (sd_j > 0 ? 1 : 0)
> > - extent = regionExtent · Π over j < count of factor_j
> >
> > c_j and h_j are the centre and half-size of the measured, ungrown bound. g = `quiet.grow` and F = `field.mask.quietFeather`, both in render pixels. On the grown boundary and inside it, extent is exactly 0. Unused slots are inert through `count` only. A zero-size rectangle is not empty: it carves a disc of radius g.
>
> **Why**
> - DSN-023's "grown by 8 dp" is a clearance: no field within 8 dp of text or a control. The rounded form is exactly that set; the square form adds corner area nobody asked for.
> - The feather contours become concentric rounded rectangles with no corner kink, which fits the pill-and-round language.
> - It costs the same.
> - QA-023 holds the same way, because the legal text box sits at least 8 dp inside in every direction. And 8 dp is far wider than the upsampling half-width at renderScale 0.33.
