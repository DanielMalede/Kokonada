> ## Ruling 2: the nine readings
>
> **DEC text:**
>
> > **1. Node colour: CORRECTED.**
> > - A bead node's colour is mix(its turned field colour, its steady halo colour, w_i), with **w_i = smoothstep(−0.05, 0, h_i − k_field)**. h_i is that node's own halo; k_field is the pixel's field weight after m and extent.
> > - A rest lobe's colour is its turned field colour.
> > - The topTwoSoft weight is **e_i = extent(x)·f_i + λ·h_i**.
> > - Consequence: wherever extent(x) = 0, a pixel depends on the halos alone and is byte-identical across t, s, c, the breath and the shimmer. Those places are the ring and the moat in every direction, Linen's dish face, Basin's screen ground, and every quiet zone.
> > - The per-node form and "stays on the node's own hue" are confirmed.
> >
> > **2. Neutral placement: CONFIRMED.**
> > - A neutral placement is node R0. Its field colour is the tensed rest glow, and its halo is the steady rest glow at the halo peak.
> > - It has a lobe only where `field.lobe.neutralLobe` = 1 (Abyss, Basin). In Linen it adds a halo and no lobe. What the rest lobes do then is item 6.
> >
> > **3. Grain region: CONFIRMED.**
> > - `field.grain.inside` applies where d < ringInner: the lens, the dish, the floor.
> > - `field.grain.ground` applies everywhere else the shader draws: the ring (lip), the moat, the screen ground.
> >
> > **4. Halos unwarped: CONFIRMED.**
> > - Halos use the unwarped distance |q − b_i| from the bead centre, with static bead and halo radii.
> > - They are clipped at d ≤ 1.
> >
> > **5. Lobe centre: CONFIRMED.**
> > - c_i = α·b_i + β·b̂_i + drift_slot(t, s).
> > - b_i is the bead centre in plate units: the unit-disc position × (1 − r_bead/R).
> > - b̂_i = b_i/|b_i|, and 0 when b_i = 0.
> > - The drift is in plate units.
> >
> > **6. Rest lobes fade: CONFIRMED, with one correction.**
> > - Correction: restFade's target is 1 while no placement contributes a lobe, and 0 otherwise. "No lobe" means no placements at all, or only neutral placements where `neutralLobe` = 0.
> > - The change is one crossfade through the uniforms, never a layer opacity. Over its eased progress u (clamped to [0, 1]), the incoming node's fade is u and the outgoing node's fade is 1 − u².
> > - It runs both ways: from rest to the first lobe-bearing placement, and from the last one's removal back to rest. A removed placement stays in the frame until its fade reaches 0.
> > - It runs on the placement fade's own clock. Under reduced motion and in the still tier it runs over `motion.fadeReduced` (150 ms, linear).
> > - Plan §B4's unit test becomes: "The still tier draws once at t* and runs no frame callback while the state is unchanged; a state change runs one `motion.fadeReduced` ramp and stops."
> >
> > **7. Placement chroma: CORRECTED.**
> > - **p = clamp((r − placement.neutralRadius) / (placement.rampEnd − placement.neutralRadius), 0, 1)**, with r = |(x, y)| on the unit disc, clamped to 1.
> > - A node's field and halo chroma is the anchor tier's chroma × p × `bead.chromaScale`, at the anchor tier's L. DSN-017's lightness ramp applies to the bead fill only.
> > - The node is a function of (x, y): neutral iff r < `placement.neutralRadius`; otherwise the quadrant by sign (x ≥ 0 pleasant, y ≥ 0 more energy).
> > - The builder computes the node, or asserts that the node passed in equals it.
> >
> > **8. The still: CONFIRMED.**
> > - The still is the full tier's law at t = `field.stillTime`, b = 0.5 and no shimmer.
> > - It uses `field.tiers.full.octaves` (3) at `field.tiers.full.renderScale` (0.5), and is drawn once.
> >
> > **9. Quiet zones and plate shadows: CORRECTED.**
> > - Both are terms of the one field law (E2, DSN-023), not B6 layers.
> > - **Where they land:** their mechanism goes in B4, in `fieldLaw.ts`, `fieldShader.ts`, `uniforms.ts` and `fieldFrame.ts`, with real-Skia agreement and mutation proofs on synthetic geometry, and in `FieldFallback`'s clip. B6 only measures the rects and passes them in.
> > - Neither is ever a separate layer, scrim, mask or opacity overlay.
> > - **Quiet zones:** extent(x) = regionExtent(x) · Π_j q_j(x).
> >   - q_j = smoothstep(0, F, sd_j(x)) when F > 0, or [sd_j(x) > 0] when F = 0.
> >   - sd_j is the signed distance in dp to rect j grown by `quiet.grow` (8 dp). F = `field.mask.quietFeather`.
> >   - There are up to 6 rects; unused slots are inert.
> >   - extent is exactly 0 inside every grown rect.
> > - **Plate shadows:** multiply the local ground's L before the composite: G.L ← G.L·(1 − Σ strength·P), summed over the face's `plate.shadow` and `plate.shadow2`.
> >   - depth > 0 (outer): P = 1 − smoothstep(−blur, blur, |x − c − o| − R), outside the rim only.
> >   - depth < 0 (inner): P = smoothstep(−blur, blur, |x − c − o| − R_face), on the face only.
> >   - o = (0, offsetY) dp, downward on screen; distances are in dp.
> >   - Strength is signed (a negative value lifts), and the shadow is static.
> >   - The never-red 8-bit sweep adds each shadowed ground at L·(1 − Σ strength).
