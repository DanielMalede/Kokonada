## The question, as each source asked it (history)

### j1-r1-compliance.md · the verdict, two standalone HALTs · line 12

- **HALT-B (store-policy breach, reachable today):** a delayed `state-vector-recompute` job outlives account deletion and consent withdrawal, then re-creates `MedicalProfile.stateVector` for the deleted or withdrawn user. Detail under Q1 and BE-087.

### j1-r1-compliance.md · Q1, Google Play, also required before the Play data-deletion answers, item 1 · lines 55–60

1. **HALT-B / BE-087, reachable with the shipped client today.**
   - Producers: `baselines.js:349-354` (5 s delay) and `metricStore.js:190-195` (60 s delay) queue `{userId}`.
   - Neither deletion nor withdrawal purges that job (`userJobPurge.js:36-38`; exempted at `userJobPurge.guards.test.js:58-60`).
   - When it runs after an account deletion, `stateVector.worker.js:70` calls `upsertStateVector`, which does `findOneAndUpdate(…, { upsert: true })` (`medicalProfileService.js:218-234`). That re-creates a `MedicalProfile` holding the encrypted inferred-state label, and `MedicalProfile.js` has no TTL. So the document stays for good under the deleted account's ID.
   - After a withdrawal it re-creates the same document, plus `bio:baseline:` (24 h, `baselines.js:296`).
   - This breaks the Play deletion rule quoted above. `#verify`: on real Mongo, sync a health batch, delete the account within 60 s, then count `MedicalProfile` for that id after 70 s.

### j1-r1-compliance.md · NEW-FINDING (BE-087, P1; HALT-B) · lines 199–202

- **NEW-FINDING (BE-087, P1; HALT-B, gates the Play data-deletion answers and the iOS submission):** a `state-vector-recompute` job delayed 5 to 60 s (`baselines.js:349-354`, `metricStore.js:190-195`) is purged by neither account deletion nor withdrawal.
  - When it runs, it re-creates `MedicalProfile.stateVector` by upsert (`medicalProfileService.js:218-234`; no TTL), and `bio:baseline:` after a withdrawal.
  - Fix: register the queue in `USER_JOB_QUEUES`, plus a worker-side check that the user exists and has a current grant before any write (the BE-028 mark-first pattern); then declare it.
  - `#verify` on real Mongo, as described under Q1.
