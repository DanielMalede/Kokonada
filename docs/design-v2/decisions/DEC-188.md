### The scribe's notes

- what the ruling is: only that the product is free for now ("רק חינמי"). "No advertising" and "no sale of data" are not his ruling here.
- the scribe's proposed application wording, which the owner did not rule: pre-revenue, no price, no advertising, and user data, Garmin data included, never sold, never given to advertisers and never used for anything but the user's own experience. Each clause past "free" stands only as a statement of the draft policy or of the code, and goes into the application only if the page and the code keep it true and the owner approves the answer sheet (`DAN-103`).
- what forces a new application and a new policy version: any price, subscription, advertising, sponsorship or data licensing. Garmin's commercial terms (its Enterprise tier, described by its own page as "commercial terms ... tailored") are a separate question that begins when the product earns money or passes the Starter tier's user limit.
