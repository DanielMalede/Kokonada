### The scribe's notes

- what the application may say: pre-revenue, no price, no advertising, and user data, Garmin data included, never sold, never given to advertisers and never used for anything but the user's own experience. Each of those clauses is already a statement of the policy page or of the code; none may go into the application unless the page and the code keep it true.
- what forces a new application and a new policy version: any price, subscription, advertising, sponsorship or data licensing. Garmin's commercial terms (its Enterprise tier, described by its own page as "commercial terms ... tailored") are a separate question that begins when the product earns money or passes the Starter tier's user limit.
