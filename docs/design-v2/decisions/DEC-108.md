### pr-sentry.md · the opening finding · lines 1–9

Sentry events no longer carry credentials, request bodies, query strings, client IPs or console lines. Before this change, with `sendDefaultPii: false` and our existing `beforeSend`, events sent to Sentry could carry:
- the app JWT or the watch `whr_` token;
- cookies;
- the Garmin webhook's permanent `?secret=`;
- login, refresh and SSO request bodies (passwords, refresh tokens, Apple and Google ID tokens);
- the socket.io CONNECT token;
- the client IP;
- Engine.IO's raw request headers and session id;
- the app's own log lines, including emotion taps, heart-rate-derived tempo targets and user ids.
