> # CTA amendments: all four accepted, with one clarifying clause on the press rule
>
> | Amendment | Verdict | Reason |
> |---|---|---|
> | (1) Show | **ACCEPT**, verbatim | Netting turns "remove, then place" into a swap. A rule that waits for the "first placement admitted" would leave the CTA hidden but pressable. |
> | (2) Tint | **ACCEPT**, verbatim | The tint must never come from a tap the user has removed. If the latest admitted placement is neutral, its tint is the neutral fill (DSN-018). |
> | (3) Hide latch | **ACCEPT**, verbatim | Without it the show ramp would raise the CTA again on the next frame, so it would flicker at the frame rate. |
> | (4) Press and accessibility | **ACCEPT, plus one clause** | Append: "'Shown' means from the start of its show ramp. A CTA that is fading in may be pressed." This stops anyone reading "shown" as "at full opacity", which would make the CTA ignore presses while it is clearly visible. |
> | E6 text | **ACCEPT**, verbatim | |
>
> With these, DEC text (3) reads as below.
>
> > **DEC (the CTA and the ledger), as amended.** The CTA's appearance follows the field's admitted state, except for its hide.
> > - **Show:** at each admitted start, if the CTA is not shown and the admitted target holds a placement that is live in the store, the CTA shows on that crossfade's ramp (incoming u), from its current opacity.
> > - **Tint:** the tint of the latest admitted placement that is live in the store; if there is none, the tint holds. It changes through `mixSafe` on the admitted crossfade's ramp: `motion.settle`, or `motion.fadeReduced` (linear) under reduced motion.
> > - **Hide:** when the store's last placement is removed, the CTA hides at once from its current opacity, over `motion.respond` (`motion.fadeReduced` under reduced motion), without waiting for admission. A hide latches: from the hide until the next admitted start, nothing raises the CTA's opacity.
> > - **Press:** the CTA is pressable, and exposed to accessibility, only while the store holds at least one tap and the CTA is shown and not hiding. "Shown" means from the start of its show ramp; a CTA that is fading in may be pressed. A press always commits the store's latest taps, and the ledger never supplies the payload. The ≤3-tap payload, the three lanes and `screenToCircumplex` are unchanged.
> > - **Bound:** shows and tint changes begin only at admitted starts, which are at least 600 ms apart, and each hide needs a prior show. In any 1 s window the CTA makes at most 5 changes, which is at most 2 general flashes. Resilience's E6 pins this, including its five planted cases.
> > - DEC-016 and DSN-018 stand; the CTA is never set to a fixed neutral tint to meet this bound.

### The counter it amends · v1b-B4-designer-final-ledger.md · lines 8, 14, 16–20 and 39–47 (handed back 2026-10-06T11:45:27Z)

> - **(3) The CTA:** I counter resilience's fix with a hybrid. The show and the tint follow the ledger; the hide follows the store at once.

> | (3) CTA fully on the ledger | **COUNTER**: show and tint on the ledger, hide on the store at once | See below. |

> **Why the CTA needs a hybrid**
> - **Against a fully ledger-driven CTA:** it would sit visible but inert for up to 2.4 s after the last bead is removed. It would also trail the bead on an undo, the most common quick correction.
> - **Against a neutral CTA:** DEC-016 and DSN-018 are the owner's decisions, and the reactive tint is part of the design language.
> - **Against a slow tint alone:** an eased 600 ms tween re-targeted every 143 ms still swings about 65% of ΔY 0.19, around 0.12, which is flash-sized. It also does nothing about show and hide.
> - **The hybrid's flash bound:** shows and tint changes happen only at crossfade starts, which are at least 600 ms apart, and every hide needs a prior show. That allows at most 5 changes, so at most 2 flashes, in any 1 s window.

> ## DEC text (3): the CTA and the ledger
>
> > The CTA's appearance follows the field's admitted state, except for its hide.
> > - **Show:** the CTA appears when the ledger admits the first placement, on that crossfade's ramp (incoming u).
> > - **Tint:** it takes the latest admitted placement's tint, changing through `mixSafe` on the admitted crossfade's ramp. That ramp is `motion.settle`, or `motion.fadeReduced` (linear) under reduced motion. So the field, its halos and the CTA change together, at most once per `motion.settle.duration`.
> > - **Hide:** when the store's last placement is removed, the CTA hides at once from its current opacity, over `motion.respond` (`motion.fadeReduced` under reduced motion), without waiting for admission. The commit action is never shown as available when there is nothing to commit (DEC-032).
> > - **Press:** the CTA is pressable only while the store holds at least one tap. A press always commits the store's latest taps, and the ledger never supplies the payload. The ≤3-tap payload, the three lanes and `screenToCircumplex` are unchanged.
> > - **Bound:** shows and tint changes happen only at crossfade starts, and each hide needs a prior show. In any 1 s window the CTA makes at most 5 changes, which is at most 2 general flashes. E6 pins this with two planted cases that must fail: a tint that follows the store directly, and a show that follows the store directly.
> > - DEC-016 and DSN-018 stand; the CTA is never set to a fixed neutral tint to meet this bound.

## The agents' agreement, verbatim

### The resilience-auditor · v1b-B4-resilience-cta.md · lines 3–66 (handed back 2026-10-06T11:47:59Z)

> ## CTA hybrid: ACCEPT, with four amendments
> The bound "at most 5 changes, at most 2 general flashes in any 1 s window" is correct, but only once the four amendments below are in the DEC text. Without amendments 1 and 2, two of the cases you asked about fail. I ran nothing; the timelines below are worked by hand from the tokens.
>
> ### Why the bound holds
> - **Starts:** admitted starts are at least 600 ms apart, so any closed 1 s window holds at most 2.
> - **Shows and tint changes:** these begin only at starts, so at most 2 per window.
> - **Hides:** a hide needs the CTA to be showing, so there is at most one hide per show in the window, plus one for a show from before it. That makes at most 3.
> - **Total:** the worst sequence is hide, show, hide, show, hide: 5 changes, 2 flashes.
> - **J/R-alternating storm:** with no hides, tint changes happen only at starts, so at most 1 flash.
> - **Toggle storm at 143 ms, settle:**
>
> | t (ms) | What happens |
> |---|---|
> | 0 | Show |
> | 143 | Hide, from opacity ≈ 0.67 |
> | 286 | P2 placed; it waits |
> | 429 | P2 removed; nothing changes |
> | 572 | P3 placed |
> | 600 | Start; the netted swap P1→P3; show |
> | 715 | Hide |
>
>   - That is 4 changes in [0, 1000], so 2 flashes.
> - **Planted cases:** a tint that follows the store, and a show that follows the store, each reach 3 flashes per second at 7 taps/s. Both planted cases fail as intended.
>
> ### The cases you asked about
> 1. **A hide followed at once by a new tap: show liveness (HIGH, as written).**
>    - The draft says the CTA "appears when the ledger admits the first placement".
>    - Netting turns "remove P1, then place P3" into a swap, so the ledger never admits a *first* placement.
>    - The CTA would stay hidden until the field passes back through rest.
>    - Meanwhile "pressable while the store holds a tap" makes it an **invisible but pressable commit button**, which TalkBack can also focus.
>    - A related problem: with Linen's 2-leaving limit, the first admitted crossfade can leave a removed placement in the target. The "latest admitted placement" tint would then come from a tap the user removed.
> 2. **A hide that starts during a show ramp (HIGH if unlatched).**
>    - If the ledger's ramp keeps writing opacity = u after a store-driven hide, the CTA shows again on the next frame. It would flicker at the frame rate.
>    - The hide must latch.
>    - A show that starts during a hide must start from the current opacity; otherwise opacity steps.
>
> ### Exact amendments to DEC text (3)
> - **Show (replaces the bullet):** "At each admitted start, if the CTA is not shown and the admitted target holds a placement that is live in the store, the CTA shows on that crossfade's ramp (incoming u), from its current opacity."
> - **Tint (replaces 'the latest admitted placement's tint'):** "the tint of the latest admitted placement that is live in the store; if there is none, the tint holds."
> - **Hide (add):** "A hide latches: from the hide until the next admitted start, nothing raises the CTA's opacity."
> - **Press (replaces the first sentence):** "The CTA is pressable, and exposed to accessibility, only while the store holds at least one tap and the CTA is shown and not hiding."
>
> ### E6, exact text
> "E6, the CTA under the ledger.
> - **Setup:** a pure `ctaState(storeLog, ledger, nowMs, mode)` returns { opacity, tint, pressable, accessible } and is sampled every 1 ms.
> - **Storms:** E3's storms plus toggleJR (place and remove the latest, alternating the J and R quadrants), in every direction, face and mode, with taps every {17, 50, 100, 143, 167, 250} ms, for 3 s and for 10 s.
> - **Flash bound:** take the CTA fill's relative luminance (its tint composited over the face's ground at its opacity, as 8-bit sRGB). It shows at most 2 general flashes (`worstFlashesPerSecond`) and at most 5 changes of 0.1 or more in any 1 s window.
> - **Shows and tints:** shows and tint changes begin only at admitted starts. Every hide begins at a store-empty event while the CTA is shown.
> - **Latch:** inject a hide at every 10 ms offset (0–600 ms) into a show ramp. From the hide until the next admitted start, opacity never rises.
> - **No step:** opacity moves at most its ramp's steepest slope per ms (show: settle or fadeReduced; hide: respond or fadeReduced). A show starts from the current opacity.
> - **Liveness:** once an admitted start's target holds a store-live tap, the CTA is at opacity 1 by that start plus its ramp. It is fully hidden within one hide duration of the store emptying.
> - **Press:** `pressable || accessible` implies the store holds a tap and the CTA is shown and not hiding.
> - **Tint truth:** the tint always belongs to a placement that is admitted and live in the store.
> - **Planted cases, each must fail:**
>   - the tint follows the store;
>   - the show follows the store;
>   - no hide latch (the ramp rewrites opacity);
>   - the show fires only on 'first placement admitted' (it fails liveness after a netted hide followed by a tap);
>   - the CTA is pressable while hidden."
>
> ### Status
> - The CTA flash bound becomes **HARDENED** once E6 has been watched failing first against the five planted cases.
> - The S7 device capture still has to include the CTA region.
> - **Stability:** 8.5/10 with the amendments. It is 6/10 if the hybrid ships as written, because of the invisible pressable CTA and the unlatched hide.

The designer accepted each of the four amendments and the E6 text in its final text, quoted above (lines 5–11).

### The resilience-auditor · v1b-B4-resilience-admission.md · its follow-up verdict and the COUNTER · lines 6 and 46–52 (handed back 2026-10-06T11:43:27Z)

> - **COUNTER on one coupled surface:** the CTA. It is not part of the bead layer, but the same tap drives it, and it reopens the flash-rate risk.

> **COUNTER (HIGH): the CTA is outside every serialization.**
> - It shows on the first placement and hides on the last removal (`V1_DIRECTIONS_DESIGNER.md:130`, DEC-032).
> - Its tint is the most recent placement's (`:777`, DSN-018).
> - It is 56 dp tall. If it is full-width (about 328 dp; I found no width token), it covers about 18,000 dp²: roughly 0.85× the threshold at 30 cm and 1.2× at 25 cm.
> - On the dark face, joy core #E3B667 (Y ≈ 0.51) against reflective core #619DDA (Y ≈ 0.32) gives ΔY ≈ 0.19.
> - So tapping alternately in J and R at 7 taps/s, or toggling place/remove, flashes the CTA about 3.5 times a second.
> - Fix (a designer ruling, since the CTA would lag the taps): the CTA's visibility and tint follow the ledger's admitted state, with the same spacing of at least 600 ms. The voice line's glyph ink is far below the threshold, so ACCEPT it, but sample it in S7.
