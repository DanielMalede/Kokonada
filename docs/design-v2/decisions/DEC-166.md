### Q1 · the amended DEC (fallback field clip) · lines 12–36 and 44

> ## Q1: the amended DEC (replaces r3 §2's DEC)
>
> > **DEC (fallback field clip), amended 2026-11-11.** FieldFallback draws the field in one region of the plate, chosen by the direction's `field.mask`:
> > - the face (d < ringInner) when `field.mask.face` > 0: Abyss, and fixture-inner's floor;
> > - otherwise the outside beyond the moat (d > moatOuter) when `field.mask.outside` > 0: fixture-outer;
> > - otherwise nowhere.
> >
> > It never draws both. On Abyss the field shows only through the lens; nothing beyond the ring is lit in the fallback.
> >
> > From that region the clip removes every grown quiet zone whose presence is not exactly 0 (Ruling Q2), using the law's rounded geometry (sdBox − grow, r3 §1) with no feather.
> >
> > The clip is drawn without anti-aliasing (`doAntiAlias` false). Each device pixel is decided at its centre by the law's step test:
> > - a zone removes the pixel when sd ≤ 0 there;
> > - the face keeps it when d is below the face's bound there;
> > - the outside keeps it when d > moatOuter there.
> >
> > Straight edges therefore fall on whole device pixels, and a pixel whose centre lies exactly on a zone's grown boundary is removed.
> >
> > Where an opaque plate surface covers the clip's boundary, the clip extends 1 dp under it, so the visible edge is that surface's own anti-aliased edge. The face's bound is ringInner·R + 1 dp, under the ring.
> >
> > No layer paints the ground colour over the field, with or without anti-aliasing. In the fallback the moat is simply where the clip leaves out the field; it is never an annulus drawn on top.
> >
> > On Abyss no clip edge shows: the face's boundary lies under the ring, and in the hero's layout no quiet zone comes within 44 dp of the lens. Where the region is the outside, the stair-step on the moat circle and on zone corners is accepted in the shader-failure state, as banding is.
>
> Why: r3 contradicted itself on Abyss; in the hero layout (384 dp wide, plate 304 dp) both readings light only narrow slivers and crescents (28 dp beyond the moat each side; voice zone's grown edge 4 dp above the moat top, CTA 12 dp below its bottom), full strength where the shader dims them by its 24 dp moat feather and 40 dp quiet feather. Dropping the moat (b) breaks the rim's 3:1 on its outer side (QA-025): glow beside the rim ≈ 1.3:1 at the rim's foot on dark (calm, k 0.539), 2.6:1 at its top on light; bare moat 4.2:1 and 3.3:1 (OKLab L³ as luminance, to be measured). The face boundary is the only one an opaque surface covers: no aliased edge, no sliver, no zone cut, every text row on bare ground (DSN-023, QA-023), D6's lens composition kept. Cost: the spill around the plate is lost in this state. No per-direction code; both fixtures work unchanged.
>
> Owner-visible: YES, the main choice.

### Q2 · presence in the fallback clip · lines 46–54 and 58

> ## Q2: presence in the fallback clip
>
> > **Ruling Q2: HARD.** In FieldFallback a quiet zone escapes removal only while its presence is exactly 0. Any other presence removes the whole grown zone: every p > 0, and also a missing, non-finite or negative value.
> >
> > A measured rect that is not finite, or has a negative size, cannot be removed. The fallback then draws no field at all: the ground, the face material and the ring only.
> >
> > The fallback removes every rect in `layout.quietRects`, with no clamp to QUIET_SLOTS. A rect beyond the presence list counts as present. FieldFallback never throws on any of these.
>
> Why: not opacity (the sRGB mix the DEC keeps out); not the law's scaled stops (under DSN-023 a zone's content draws only at p = 1, so nothing to protect at 0 < p < 1). Invisible on Abyss. One clip, no extra draw; a one-shot change outside QA-068; malformed input errs toward legibility.
>
> Owner-visible: no.

### Q3 · who draws the plate surfaces · lines 60–76 and 86

> ## Q3: who draws the plate surfaces
>
> > **Ruling Q3.** FieldFallback draws inside its one Skia canvas, in this order:
> > 1. `color.<face>.ground` over the whole canvas;
> > 2. the face material: `plate.face`, a disc of radius ringInner·R + 1 dp;
> > 3. the field gradient, clipped by DEC (fallback field clip);
> > 4. the ring: `plate.ring`, the annulus from ringInner to the rim, opaque, anti-aliased on both edges.
> >
> > Then E6 draws the rim stroke, the words and the beads, unchanged. E6 never draws the ring, in either path.
> >
> > Every surface is its flat token colour. There is no face vignette (Abyss dark's −0.18), no centre lift (Abyss light's +0.03), no plate shadow, no halo, no grain and no dither. The moat is not drawn; the ground shows there.
> >
> > The canvas is sRGB, under the same contract as FieldSurface (Fork 2). No React Native View paints a colour (r2 §3c item 5, UI-204).
>
> Why: vignette/lift are plate-centred, the gradient lobe-centred; one opaque gradient cannot carry both, and the alternatives are the sRGB-mix class or a post-multiply that darkens the glow. The ring is drawn so the fallback has a plate surface and the lens an edge; anti-aliased because the lens edge is the focal circle; with the 1 dp extension its fringe mixes `plate.ring` with the gradient only.
>
> Never red measured, not assumed: the never-red sweep samples the fallback's real CanvasKit pixels, Abyss dark and light; nodes J, C, R, I at p ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1} plus neutral and rest; every gradient pixel and every pixel of the ring's inner fringe; each against QA-065's pass rule (hue outside the band, or chroma below the floor).
>
> Owner-visible: yes. Plate whole, lens edge smooth; lens a little flatter (dark edge L 0.150 vs the shader's 0.123; light centre 0.970 vs 1.000).

### Q5 · the gradient's pose · lines 94–106 and 115

> ## Q5: the gradient's pose
>
> > **Ruling Q5.** The fallback draws the field's mean pose at s = 0 and c = null: the breath at its mean level b = 0.5, the drift at its mean 0, no shimmer, no warp, never genesis.
> >
> > (i) **Radius:** radiusScale × `field.lobe.radius` × (1 + `field.breath.ampRadius`/2), which is radiusScale × the builder's still lobe radius at s = 0. Abyss, both faces: 1.35 × 0.95 × 1.04 = 1.3338 R.
> >
> > (ii) **Centre:** the lobe-centre rule at zero drift. For a placement, lobeAnchor(x, y, r_bead, centreAlpha, centreBeta), which on Abyss is the bead. For a rest lobe, the table's centre. Never the still's drifted centre at t*.
> >
> > (iii) **Which placement:** the newest placement (the last tap) that carries a lobe, meaning any node other than neutral, or a neutral where `field.lobe.neutralLobe` > 0. If none carries one, the rest state.
> >
> > (iv) **The rest state:** one gradient for node R0 (the face's `field.restGlow`), centred at the mean of the face's rest-lobe centres, with the same radius and stops. Abyss dark: (−0.30, 0.35) R. Abyss light: (0.025, 0.025) R.
> >
> > On a change of placement or presence the fallback redraws at once. It never crossfades: a crossfade of two opaque gradients is an alpha mix.
>
> Owner-visible: (ii) yes (glow concentric with the newest bead, only that bead glows); (iv) yes on light at rest (even tint); (i) not in practice; (iii) no.

### The developer's settled items · lines 117–121

Keep: doAntiAlias true on the clip; a zone not removed; no 1 dp extension (the face bound under the ring, Abyss and fixture-inner); clip not snapped; a zone feathered; wrong ground (stops over a vignetted face, or over the ring).
