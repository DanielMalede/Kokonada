### v1b-B4-law-round2-designer.md · Items 2 and 3, the selection and blend law · lines 66–102

> ## 2 + 3. The selection and blend law, ruled together
>
> **Three causes:**
> - **(a) The seam jump.** This is my T1 error: `w2 = max(0, e2 − e3); t = w2/(e1 + w2)` jumps by e3/(2e − e3) wherever the two strongest swap.
> - **(b) The unbounded potential at a lobe's centre.** The potential is r²/max(d², 1e-6). At fade 0.001 a lobe still has f ≈ 900·r² near its centre, so it keeps both the colour selection and m = 1 until fade is exactly 0. That is the 500→600 ms step in the probe (Y 0.252 → 0.061). λ = 1000 does the same inside a fading halo.
> - **(c) The same-spot bump.** u + (1 − u²) peaks at 1.25. On the steep part of m that gives the ±0.14 Y swing each swap. Separately, H = max(h) dips to 0.618 for two halos at the same spot.
>
> **The replacement law.** fieldLaw.ts and the SkSL stay twins.
>
> - **L1, the lobe with a core:**
>   - ρ_i = r_i² / (|q′ − c_i|² + κ·r_i²) and f_i = fade_i·ρ_i.
>   - **κ = `field.lobe.core` = 0.04**, a new base token. It replaces the 1e-6 clamp.
>   - Visible effect: the m = 1 and m = 0 contours move in by about 3% and 1% of r_L. Nothing else changes.
> - **L2, the halo:**
>   - η_i = haloWeight(|q − b_i|)/haloPeak inside the rim; 0 outside the rim and for rest lobes.
>   - h_i = fade_i·haloPeak·η_i, as today.
>   - **H = min(haloPeak, Σ h_i)**, replacing max.
> - **L3, the selection weight:**
>   - e_i = fade_i·(extent·ρ_i + λ·η_i).
>   - **λ = `field.graph.lambda`: 1000 → 25**, which is 1/κ: a halo at its peak weighs the same as a lobe at its core.
> - **L4, ranking:** e1 ≥ e2 ≥ e3. Ties go to the lower slot, though the result no longer depends on tie order.
> - **L5, the pair:**
>   - w1 = e1 − e3 and w2 = e2 − e3.
>   - t = w2/(w1 + w2) when w1 > 0, otherwise 0. So t ∈ [0, ½].
>   - On a straight edge: P = c1 + (c2 − c1)·t.
>   - On a waypoint edge: P = c1 + (N12 − c1)·2t, with N12 = ((c1.L + c2.L)/2, 0, 0).
> - **L6, the fade near the triple point:**
>   - β = smoothstep(0, θ, w1/e1), with **θ = `field.graph.tripleFade` = 0.15**, a new base token.
>   - N̄ = (Σ e_i·c_i.L / Σ e_i, 0, 0) over all six slots.
>   - colour = (N̄.L + (P.L − N̄.L)·β, β·P.a, β·P.b).
>   - Where eMax = 0, the colour is the ground, as today.
> - **L7, the crossfade** (amends designer 2.6; JS only, computed once per crossfade by the ledger):
>   - incoming fade = u; outgoing fade_j = 1 − u^(1+ω_j).
>   - ω_j = smoothstep(0, ν·r_L, δ_j), where δ_j is the distance from the outgoing lobe's undrifted centre to the nearest incoming undrifted centre in the same crossfade. With no incoming lobe, ω_j = 1.
>   - r_L = `field.lobe.radius`, without the breath.
>   - **ν = `field.crossfade.overlapFar` = 1.25**, a new base token.
>   - A halo uses its lobe's fade. The rule is the same in settle and reduced motion. Starts stay at least motion.settle.duration apart in every mode.

### The architect · v1b-B4-law-round2-architect.md · the bottom line · lines 7–20 (handed back 2026-10-07T09:06:52Z)

> ## Bottom line (input SHA `52cb7c8`)
> 1. **Item 2 is two defects, and only one of them is in the selection law.** Ruling 2 and 3 together covers half of item 2.
>    - **2a, the rest-lobe tail.** The lobe kernel `f = fade·r²/max(d², 1e-6)` (`fieldLaw.ts:322`, shader `lobe()` at `fieldShader.ts:73-76`) has no real ceiling. So at a lobe centre the pixel does not change smoothly as fade approaches 0: it jumps when fade reaches 0.
>    - The same `f` feeds both the weight (`fSum` → m, `fieldLaw.ts:323,335`) and the selection (`e`, `:340`). **A selection-only fix cannot remove the step.** The fix has to go in the kernel.
>    - **2b, the same-spot swap bump.** After 600 ms the probe has no rest lobe and two calm lobes at the same tap (`[l0.79 l0.45]`) with identical colours. Selection does not matter there, and with e3 = 0 the item-3 formula equals today's anyway. The bump is the crossfade sum u + (1 − u²) ≤ 1.25 feeding m. That lives in `crossfade.ts` / `placementLedger.ts`, not in the selection law.
>    - **Arithmetic, not a measurement:** starts are ≥ 600 ms apart. A start whose transient at a point is one rise plus one fall gives at most 4 changes in any 1 s window, which is 2 flashes. Getting 3 flashes needs one start that makes two opposing pairs at one point, and the 2a tail does exactly that (Y 0.183 → 0.252 → 0.061 across 400–600 ms).
>    - **Inference to measure:** fixing 2a alone probably brings E4 to ≤ 2. 2b is then a question of margin and look, not a blocker.
> 2. **Item 3 works, with two constraints.**
>    - (i) The triple-point fallback N3 must be symmetric in the nodes. Otherwise the discontinuity just moves onto the e2 = e3 lines near the triple point.
>    - (ii) A NaN guard is required at an exact triple tie: 0·NaN is NaN in both TS and SkSL.
> 3. **Item 4 goes in the shader (the law), over one render pixel.** No new uniform. A one-device-pixel edge is physically impossible from a half-resolution render without a second draw. That is a FieldView option I cost below; the designer decides.
> 4. **Item 7: a presence float per slot, with zones on fixed named slots.** +6 floats, +2 declarations. Presence is per-frame `FieldState`, not `FieldGeometry`.
> 5. **Item 8: split the scenes file and add a `sweepsSkia` project to `jest.sweeps.config.js`.** The `ci.yml` run line and filter stay as they are; only comments change.
> 6. **Uniforms: 157 → 165 floats, 46 → 48 declarations.** That is about 21% of the GLES 3.0 floor of 224 fragment vectors.
