## The designer's ruling, verbatim

### v1b-B4-designer-rulings.md · Ruling 1 · lines 18–30 (handed back 2026-10-06T07:40:36Z)

> ## Ruling 1: the dither. Option (a): keep the token, amend the plan, re-pin the test
>
> **DEC text:**
>
> > The field dither is defined in OKLab, not in sRGB. `field.dither.amplitude` stays 0.003922 (±1/255 of OKLab L). It is one scalar per pixel from the static blue-noise tile, added before the sRGB encode and rounded once.
> >
> > Its 8-bit footprint follows each channel's local slope σ. σ is the channel's unrounded 8-bit change over ±amplitude of L at the pixel's undithered colour. No channel moves by more than ⌈σ⌉ LSB.
> >
> > On every dark-face ground σ < 1. There no channel moves by more than 1 LSB, and the dither is active.
> >
> > On bright or saturated pixels a channel may move by 2 LSB or more. This is accepted: the change is still 1/255 of OKLab L, about a fifth of one just-noticeable difference (ΔE_OK 0.02, the CSS Color 4 figure).
> >
> > Per-pixel slope scaling (b) and a lower global amplitude (c) are rejected. The plan's "±1 LSB" is replaced by this rule.

The file's reasons follow at lines 32–60 (banding on a calm dark ground, perceived grain, consistency across 3 directions × 2 faces, GPU cost). Its point 1 is replaced by the second design critic's text, quoted below; points 2 to 4 are restated in the r2 file, lines 33–43.

### v1b-B4-designer-rulings-r2-with-forks.md · RULING 1 · lines 20–22 (handed back 2026-10-06T07:48:33Z; the later of the designer's two texts)

> ## RULING 1: the dither, plan against token
>
> > **DEC (field dither, B4/UI-068).** Option (a). The dither stays at ±`field.dither.amplitude` = 1/255 on OKLab L. It is one scalar per pixel from the static blue-noise tile, added before the sRGB encode and rounded once (DSN-032). It is defined on OKLab L, not as 8-bit channel steps. Its 8-bit footprint is whatever the sRGB encoding makes of ±1/255 of L at that colour: no more than ⌈σ⌉ LSB per channel, where σ is that channel's unrounded 8-bit change over ±1/255 of L at the pixel's undithered colour. On every dark-face ground σ < 1, so there the dither moves no channel by more than 1 LSB. On bright or saturated pixels a channel may move by 2 or more. Option (b), scaling per pixel by the slope, and option (c), a lower global amplitude, are rejected. The token value does not change; plan §B4's "±1 LSB" is amended.

### The second design critic · §1 · lines 57–77

> - **The premise holds.** σ is below 1 on every dark-face ground, with a margin of 0.035 at the top.
> - **The designer's figures are partly misquoted:**
>   - The "0.74 on Basin's floor" is the floor's green channel. Its red channel is 0.83: it sits just above the sRGB linear knee (linear 0.00317 against 0.0031308), and the dither carries it across the knee.
>   - So the range of largest-channel σ is 0.83–0.965, not 0.74–0.965.
>   - The share of pixels a flat ground's dominant channel changes is therefore at least 40–48%, not 32–48%.
>   - Basin's lip (0.958) is a near-tie with Linen's ring and should be named.
> - **Light faces** run from 1.27 (Basin's floor, L 0.86) to 1.32, not 1.29–1.31.
> - **The test premise acts as a guard.** A warm dark ground at L ≥ ~0.28 would reach σ = 1, and the premise test stops that change. Keep the designer's "stop and report".
>
> ### Rejecting (b) and (c) for banding on the signature dark ground
> Both rejections are right.
> - **(b)** scales the amplitude by min(1, 1/σ). That factor is 1 on every dark ground, so (b) does nothing there. It costs ALU on Mali/Xclipse for an invisible change on bright pixels. It would also make the dither's ΔL move as the field moves, which breaks "fixed to the screen" (DSN-032).
> - **(c)'s rationale is overstated.** The grain is ±2.7 to ±9.8 LSB on these grounds and is itself a blue-noise dither on every pixel, so banding would not visibly "return" today.
> - **The real reason to reject (c)** is that the dither must be the banding floor without help from the grain. At ±0.16–0.21 LSB it falls below the ±0.5 LSB a rectangular dither needs to make the mean continuous. Example: Abyss's lens vignette spans only about four blue levels across the lens radius. The token's ±0.85–0.89 LSB dither renders that band-free by itself; (c) would not.
>
> ### COUNTER text for the DEC (replaces rationale point 1; the DEC block stands, plus the last two paragraphs)
> > σ, the largest channel per dark-face ground: Abyss ground 0.89 (blue), ring 0.94 (blue); Linen ground 0.87, dish face 0.94, ring 0.965 (red, forward difference; the highest); Basin ground 0.91, floor 0.83 (red, which straddles the sRGB linear knee; green 0.75, blue 0.65), lip 0.96 (red). Light faces run from 1.27 to 1.32. On a flat dark ground the dominant channel changes on at least 40–48% of pixels.
> >
> > The dither is the field's banding floor on its own: it must hold with `field.grain.*` at 0. (c) is rejected for that reason. At ≈0.00085 it spans ±0.16–0.21 LSB on every dark ground, below the ±0.5 LSB a rectangular dither needs, so the floor would rest on a taste token, the grain.
> >
> > Bias is measured on a frame whose colours span many 8-bit levels: there, the mean per-channel change against the undithered render is under 0.1 LSB. (On a single flat ground the undithered render is itself off by up to 0.5 LSB, which is what the dither removes.)
