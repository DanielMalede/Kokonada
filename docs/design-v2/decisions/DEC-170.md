# DEC-170 · A one-shot fade is outside QA-068's periodic test and its repetition is inside it: one ramp per event, never a jump, serialized crossfades at least `motion.settle.duration` apart, and at most two flashes a second (V1 B4)

- status: DECIDED
- opened: 2026-10-06
- ruled: 2026-10-06
- ruling: recorded 2026-11-17; the orchestrator's ruling under DEC-142, the designer's ruling and the agents' agreement are quoted below. The scribe's English gloss: QA-068 measures repetition, not speed. A one-shot fade started by a user event (place, replace, remove) or a state change (Genesis) has no frequency and is outside the periodic 3 Hz uniform test, which covers every uniform that is a function of the clock or the breath phase. Its repetition is inside QA-068: under any input sequence no pixel of the field shows 3 or more WCAG 2.3.1 general flashes in any 1 s window. The bounds: one ramp per triggering event, never scheduled by a timer, the clock, the breath or an inferred state; a new event starts its ramp from the current fade values; the ramp lasts `motion.settle`, or `motion.fadeReduced` (150 ms, linear) under reduced motion and in the still tier; incoming u, outgoing 1 − u², and the reduced-motion outgoing slope of 13.3/s is accepted as a one-shot. Crossfades are serialized, never interrupted, and start at least `motion.settle.duration` (600 ms) apart in every mode; pending changes are netted against the store's latest placements and admitted within the slot limit; the bead layer is not serialized. A longer reduced ramp (213 ms or more) and a linear outgoing fade are rejected.

### v1b-B4-designer-rulings-r3-corners-clip-qa068.md · the short answer and §3 · lines 10 and 66–81 (handed back 2026-10-06T07:56:51Z)

> - **QA-068:** one-shot fades are outside it. I reject both the longer reduced ramp and the linear outgoing fade.

> ## 3. QA-068 against the crossfade: option (i), one-shots are outside QA-068
>
> > **DEC (one-shot fades and QA-068).** QA-068 governs periodic luminance modulation, which has a frequency. A one-shot fade has none and is outside QA-068 (DSN-032: one-shot transitions are not periodic). This covers fades triggered by a user event (place, replace, remove) or by a state change (Genesis). Field fades are bounded instead by four rules:
> > - (a) One ramp per triggering event. A fade is never scheduled by a timer, the clock, the breath or any inferred state, so fades cannot chain into an oscillation.
> > - (b) A new event during a ramp starts its ramp from the current fade values, so no fade ever jumps.
> > - (c) The ramp lasts `motion.settle` normally, and `motion.fadeReduced` (150 ms, linear u) under reduced motion and in the still tier.
> > - (d) In every mode the incoming fade is u and the outgoing fade is 1 − u².
> >
> > The builder's 3 Hz rate test covers every uniform that is a function of the clock or the breath phase. The fade inputs (placement fades and restFade) are excluded from it. They get their own test instead: monotone in u, exactly 0 and 1 at the ends, and no ramp shorter than `motion.fadeReduced`.
>
> **Why**
> - **Not a longer reduced ramp.** Anything of 213 ms or more breaches QA-010. QA-010 is a P1 obligation: "transitions are crossfades ≤150 ms", with "every transition ≤150 ms" in its pass rule. It would also contradict DEC-141's "150 ms crossfades", and it needs registry amendments for no visible gain.
> - **Not a linear outgoing fade.** With both fades linear, every lobe's f halves mid-ramp. The whole glow dips to about 40% and recovers within 150 ms. That reads as a blink, the closest thing to a flash of the three options.
> - **WCAG 2.3.1.** A flash is a pair of opposing changes. With the trailing curve, each area changes at most once per event. The exception is a small brighten-then-settle where lobes overlap, and there m saturates. So there is at most one flash per user event, far below three per second.
>
> **Fallback, only if resilience requires a literal rate bound for one-shots:** under reduced motion only, the outgoing fade becomes 1 − u^1.4, which peaks at 9.33 per second, under the 9.42 bound. Never linear. Normal mode keeps 1 − u².

### The resilience-auditor · §2, why (a) and the proposed ruling text · lines 52–67

> **Why (a), and its condition.** It needs one driver invariant, and that invariant is what actually bounds the flash rate:
> - Crossfade starts are spaced at least 600 ms (`motion.settle.duration`) apart in every mode.
> - That allows at most 2 starts in any closed 1 s window, so at most 2 flashes even if every crossfade produced a flash at some pixel.
> - 600 ms rather than 250 ms is recommended because of the overlap-band bump (finding 6). Starts above 200 ms apart would be enough only if that bump is measured below 0.1.
>
> ### Proposed ruling text (orchestrator / designer)
> > **QA-068 scope.** QA-068 measures repetition, not speed. A one-shot transition started by a user event (one monotone change per pixel, or one rise-and-fall where two lobes overlap) is not a modulation and is outside the periodic 3 Hz uniform test. Its repetition is inside QA-068: under any input sequence, no pixel of the field shows 3 or more WCAG 2.3.1 general flashes (a pair of opposing changes of ≥ 0.1 in relative luminance, the darker below 0.8) in any 1 s window. The reduced-motion outgoing slope of 13.3/s is ACCEPTED as a one-shot and pinned.
> >
> > **Designer 2.6, amended.**
> > - Crossfades are serialized. A crossfade never restarts or interrupts a running one, and every fade starts from its current value.
> > - Consecutive starts are at least `motion.settle.duration` (600 ms) apart in every motion mode. Under reduced motion and the still tier, the 150 ms linear ramp is followed by a hold.
> > - Changes that arrive meanwhile merge into the next crossfade, which swaps at most two beads (at most 5 nodes, fading ones included). A larger change runs as successive crossfades. The field shows the latest placements within 1.8 s of the last tap.
> > - The placement list and its fades come from one source, sampled once per frame on the UI thread.
> > - restFade's "no placement has a lobe" counts placements that are still fading out.
> > - Drift slots are keyed by placement identity, never by array position.
> > - The still tier runs the frame callback while a crossfade is pending or running, and stops when none is.

The third bullet's node clause is replaced by the designer's edit, and its last sentence by the resilience-auditor's convergence text, both quoted above.
