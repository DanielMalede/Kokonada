### dan015-compliance-check.md · §6, the Israel-only tester stage · lines 224–235

**The page must:**
- stay non-geofenced. Play says "non-geofenced URL", and Spotify V.12 says "before they install";
- name the Israeli controller (CH-1) and Israel's authority (CH-16);
- keep the EU-grade content (owner ruling);
- carry Hebrew, which counsel reads (`REG-260`).

**The page must not:**
- say "available only in Israel", "this policy applies only to users in Israel", or "GDPR does not apply";
- name an EU representative or a lead authority (G-4);
- say data is stored in Israel. It is not: it is in the US, Groq's other countries, and the Railway and Atlas regions.

The tester countries are a Play Console setting, not a page fact. If the orchestrator states them anyway, a change of countries must become a D5 version trigger.
