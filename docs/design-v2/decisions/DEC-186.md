### The scribe's notes

- what it answers: DAN-007's note of 2026-10-01 asked that a domain not move the policy's address while builds carrying `https://kokonada.github.io/privacy/` are installed. No such build has reached anyone but the owner (`MB-034`; DAN-015's note of 2026-10-01), so the address moves now, before any distribution (`MB-142`), and the `github.io` address is never depended on again.
- what the owner keeps for himself: the purchase and every step inside Cloudflare (DAN-007's Pause & Guide). No DNS record is added, changed or removed before he writes DONE, and none after it until `DAN-104` names it in its own Pause & Guide.
- the price: "about $10.46 a year" is the owner's figure; the registrar's checkout decides the amount, and his DONE records what was charged.
- what it does not settle: the host of the pages behind the domain (GitHub Pages under the `kokonada` organization or another host), which the owner selects; the text of the policy (`DAN-015`, DEC-189); and its publication, which stays owner class (c) under `CLAUDE.md`.
- why the domain matters beyond the policy: Google OAuth verification needs a DNS-verified Domain property that `github.io` can never be (DAN-007), and Garmin's Starter form needs a live website with the privacy policy (`DAN-103`).
