- the text: DSN-013, as the designer rewrote it (`v1b-r1-designer-rulings.md` §0, replacing `V1_DIRECTIONS_DESIGNER.md:772`):

  > Never red. A captured 8-bit pixel with h ∈ [350°, 50°] (both ends inclusive) and C ≥ 0.020 is red. No analytic colour may have h ∈ [345°, 55°] (inclusive) with C ≥ 0.015. Every authored colour and every sweep maximum stays at least 0.0005 below the floor of each band it is checked against: at most 0.0195 against the check band, at most 0.0145 against the design band (DEC-081, QA-063, QA-065).

## The agents' agreement, verbatim

### The designer · v1-designer-B2-review.md · §3 · lines 95–103 and 110–112 (handed back 2026-10-03T23:05:47Z)

> **Recommendation:** a colour at exactly the floor counts as red. Red means h is in the band and C ≥ floor; a colour passes only if h is outside the band or C < floor.
>
> **Why:**
> - It is the conservative reading of "never red".
> - It matches QA-065's existing pass rule ("chroma < floor"), so no registry entry has to change.
> - It makes the floor a ceiling that every colour must stay below, which is what a guard should express.
> - It costs nothing in practice: no token, blend or captured pixel sits exactly on 0.020 or 0.015, and the sweeps carry a margin.
>
> **Hue edges are inclusive at both ends,** as V0's `inRedBand` already does.
>
> **Band numbers: confirmed for ratification as they are in the base file.**
> - Check band, applied to captured 8-bit pixels: hue [350°, 50°], floor 0.020.
> - Design band, applied to float analytic colours: hue [345°, 55°], floor 0.015.

### The resilience-auditor · v1b-r1-resilience.md · the questions, item 2 · lines 113–120 (handed back 2026-10-03T23:28:31Z)

> **2. DEC-081, the boundary.**
> - The registry lists me as QA-065's author (`docs\design-v2\registry\QA-065.md:4`). Its pass rule "chroma < floor" was written so that C = floor counts as red. Recommendation: **C ≥ floor is red**, a closed band.
> - Reasons:
>   - It is fail-safe.
>   - The hue edges are already inclusive.
>   - The code's exactness (`along >= floor`) depends on a closed region.
>   - Basin dark's glyph chord sits at 0.0150 at 345°. With "C > floor", that verdict would turn on the fifth decimal.
> - Pair it with a margin rule: every sweep reports its largest in-band chroma, and every authored colour and sweep maximum keeps at least 0.0005 from both floors. Then the wording can never decide a verdict.

### The designer · v1b-r1-designer-rulings.md · the short answer and §0 · lines 8 and 13–14 (handed back 2026-10-03T23:34:15Z)

> - I accept the 0.0005 margin rule.
