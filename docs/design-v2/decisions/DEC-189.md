### The scribe's notes

- what it keeps: the pages stay static HTML readable with JavaScript off, with no cookie, no analytics, no third-party request, no form and no iframe, as `DAN-015` already requires. The publishing rule is `CLAUDE.md`'s class (c) ("every publication or change of the hosted privacy policy"); the owner's words restate it for the policy page ("פרסום הדף", in his sentence on the privacy policy), and DEC-156 adds that the policy goes live only after `DAN-050` records the sweep armed.
- the landing page's publication, the scribe's reading and not a ruling: the owner's quoted words name the policy page, not the landing page. The scribe's recommendation is that the landing page, user-visible copy that describes the product to the public, is also published only on the owner's quoted approval of the exact version (`DAN-104`); that is the scribe's, until he rules it.
- the domain's proxy, the scribe's note: if the records at Cloudflare are proxied (the orange cloud), Cloudflare can inject its Web Analytics beacon (`/cdn-cgi/rum`, `static.cloudflareinsights.com`) and Bot Fight Mode turns on JavaScript Detections, which cannot be disabled and inject scripts into the pages, which would break "בלי cookies, בלי analytics ובלי צד שלישי". So the records are DNS only (the grey cloud), or, if proxied, Web Analytics and Bot Fight Mode are off, checked by `DAN-104`'s done-when. The site host and any CDN receive every visitor's IP address and request, so the policy draft names them as recipients.
- what it does not settle: the host, which no ruling selects (DEC-186 rules the domain, not the host), the legal text the first non-owner tester sees (counsel's Q4 of the gating five, `DAN-014`), and the controller's identity (DEC-147).
- where the pages live: the public site repository (`kokonada/kokonada.github.io`, outside this repository), with its gate and tests in `Kokonada-private/site-tools`; the order of the publication steps is `PUBLISH_CHECKLIST.md` there.
