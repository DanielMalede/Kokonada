### The scribe's notes

- what it keeps: the pages stay static HTML readable with JavaScript off, with no cookie, no analytics, no third-party request, no form and no iframe, as `DAN-015` already requires. The publishing rule is `CLAUDE.md`'s class (c) ("every publication or change of the hosted privacy policy"); this ruling restates it for the landing page too: the landing page is user-visible copy that describes the product to the public, so it is published only on the owner's quoted approval of the exact version.
- what it does not settle: the host (DEC-186, which the owner decides in his own selection), the legal text the first non-owner tester sees (counsel's Q4 of the gating five, `DAN-014`), and the controller's identity (DEC-147).
- where the pages live: the public site repository (`kokonada/kokonada.github.io`, outside this repository), with its gate and tests in `Kokonada-private/site-tools`; the order of the publication steps is `PUBLISH_CHECKLIST.md` there.
