## The designer's ruling, verbatim

### v1b-B4-designer-cta-shadow.md · lines 3–12 (handed back 2026-10-07T04:13:16Z)

> # Linen light's CTA does sit on the shadowed ground: add it to the sweep, and leave the layout alone
>
> Yes, in Linen light only.
> - **The geometry.** The CTA's top edge sits `layout.gap.plateToWell` = 24 dp below the rim. The outer plate shadow reaches 34 dp below the rim (offsetY 10 + blur 24). So the top 10 dp of the CTA composite over a darkened ground.
> - **How strong it is there.** The shadow strength at 24 dp is about 0.0067 (0.06 · smoothstep(−24, 24, −14)). The second shadow reaches only 6 dp.
> - **The other directions and faces.** None is affected. Abyss has no shadows, Linen dark's shadows are zero, and Basin's are inner shadows on the floor. Above the plate the shadow reaches 14 dp, which is short of the 20 dp voice-line gap.
> - **The layout stays as it is.** The dish's soft shadow falling toward the well is intended.
>
> **DEC text (one line):**
> > The never-red CTA·ground families include each outer-shadowed ground at L·(1 − Σ strength·P), P ∈ {0, 0.5, 1}, wherever `plate.depth` > 0 and some shadow's offsetY + blur exceeds `layout.gap.plateToWell` (today only Linen light: 34 dp > 24 dp). A test derives that condition from the tokens for every direction and face, never from a hard-coded list.

The file's last line (line 14) names the token file it read, `direction.linen.tokens.json` (plate shadows at lines 112–126, `plateToWell` at line 263), by an absolute path into a user's home; the path is left out (`backend/tests/personalDataGuard.test.js`).

## The resilience-auditor's ruling, verbatim

### v1b-B4-T5d-resilience-rulings.md · §3, the CTA·ground pairs · lines 84–94 (handed back 2026-10-07T04:16:37Z)

> - **The CTA·ground pairs: not conditional on a designer ruling.**
>   - The CTA's distance from the rim depends on layout: 200% font, narrow width, landscape and large screens (the G1 matrix).
>   - A "never within ~34 dp" ruling would itself need a measured-bounds guard at every width and font scale. That costs more and protects less than sweeping two extra grounds, which is pairs-only and negligible.
>   - **Fix:** every family whose background is `c.ground` (`:365`, `:367`, and any added later) also takes each outer-shadowed ground.
>   - **Before choosing the condition**, confirm in `fieldLaw.ts`'s shadow term whether the shader darkens the ground when `mask.outside = 0`.
>     - `plateShadowsUnderField` returns null in that case, because it only asks where the field lands.
>     - If the shader darkens the ground anyway, the CTA condition must be "an outer shadow is drawn", not `plateShadowsUnderField`.
> - **Prove it fails first.**
>   - Pin the edge sweep's ground list per direction and face. Basin's pin must list the shadowed face at 0.5 and 1, and Linen light's must list the shadowed ground.
>   - `pixelBlends` for 'face lit' in Basin must grow.
>   - A mutant that drops the shadowed variants must be killed.
