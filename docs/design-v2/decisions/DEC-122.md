**What option B ships, in detail:**

- connect and callback answer `youtube_unavailable` whatever the env says;
- `buildProfile` and the workers never call YouTube;
- the retention worker revokes and purges an old link instead of refreshing it;
- a one-off script revokes and purges every old link, run after the production count.

**Recommendation: B now, D at P3, and C before the first iOS submission.**
