Whether a `versionCode` rise satisfies Art. 7(1) is counsel's CQ2 (the review's "What I am not saying").

### hx5-r1-regulatory.md · §2, "1.0 (1)" on every build · lines 29–39

**2. "1.0 (1)" on every build** (`build.gradle:94`, `versionCode 1`)
- **Before the flip it does not defeat the record.**
  - Every build from `fd68094` until the flip renders the sheet pinned to main.
  - Grants made before HX-5 carry no `appVersion`.
  - So both "1.0 (1)" and an absent value map to main's sheet.
- **At the flip it is fatal unless bumped.** If the flip build also reports "1.0 (1)", grants made with and without the link can never be told apart. O2 then collapses into O4 for that interval, and that cannot be repaired afterwards.
- **Before the flip:**
  - `versionCode` and `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` rise above every value any flag-off build has carried. `appVersion.test.ts` already forces JS and native to move together.
  - A test pins the flag to the build number.
  - No build with the flag on posts a grant to production under an old number. That includes a local build made for the archive screenshots.
  - Any later flag-off build, such as a rollback, gets its own number and its own archive entry.
