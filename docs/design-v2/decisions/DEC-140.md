### V1_AMENDMENT_COPYCHECK.md · §1, C1: the AI line is pinned (owner Round 9 Q1, REG-008) · lines 14–35

**D:658.** Old:

> - **At font scale 2.0 the content scrolls,** with the nav fixed. Taps go to the plate; vertical drags scroll.

New:

> - **The AI line is pinned at every font scale.**
>   - The status bar, the top bar and the AI line form a fixed header (`hero.header`). The nav is fixed at the bottom.
>   - Only the block [voice, plate, well] scrolls, inside `hero.scroll`, the viewport between the header and the nav.
>   - At 1.0 nothing scrolls. At 2.0 the block scrolls under the header, never over it.
>   - The field shader draws the header as a quiet zone (ground, grain, dither). The viewport clips both the plate's shader geometry and the React Native content. No fill or scrim is added.
>   - Taps go to the plate; vertical drags scroll.

**D:785 (DSN-024, second bullet).** Old:

> - Never tinted, collapsed or truncated at 2.0, and never in a live region.

New:

> - Never tinted, collapsed, truncated or scrolled out of view at any font scale (it is pinned above the scroll region), and never in a live region.
