The living field

The app today renders the shipped four-blob aurora (auroraField.ts — sky / violet / gold / pink, drifting over motion.duration.flow). The two-lobe field on this board has not been ratified and is not built. See docs/plans/DECISION_TWO_LOBE_FIELD.md.

One ambient layer behind the app that answers the body. It takes its colour from the state you are in, it breathes on your heart, and it reaches further up the screen the more activated you are. It is the only ambient element — it replaces the tide, and it never becomes four drifting blobs again.

WHAT DRIVES IT
Hue
The quadrant of the fused state on the valence×arousal circumplex — teal, amber, violet, indigo, straight from emotionAccent, as locked on 2026-07-12. Only the fused state is ever painted: the placements re-tint to it rather than holding the hue you placed, so one accent is live at a time. The never-warm constraint binds on high-arousal negative states — distress, not arousal — so amber appears when the read is genuinely bright and resourced, and never when the body contradicts it.
Breath
A regulation target, not a readout. T = 4200 + 2000 × stress — so a settled body breathes at 4200 ms (motion.duration.breath) and a stressed one stretches to 6200. Heart rate enters only as a bounded pull upward at rest. The period is monotonic in stress: it can lengthen, never shorten. The second lobe runs at a fixed 1.35 × T, which is where the drift comes from.
Reach
Arousal raises it, 62% of the screen to 86% — and depletion pulls it back down again. A wired-but-empty body gets the violet but only 68%, below the 86% of a body that is bright and resourced. That gap is the regulator, drawn.
Now Playing
The one screen where the field is a target rather than a readout. It eases from the state you arrived in toward the state the set is taking you to, over 1200 ms — so the room changes after you have arrived, not during. Everywhere else the field reports the present.
Presence
Confidence, as the field's opacity. A 0.69 read renders at 78%; a 0.95 read is fully present. The background is as sure as the engine is, and no surer — compare the hero against Genesis on this canvas.
WHERE IT APPEARS

Place, Genesis, Now Playing and Pulse — the four screens where the body is being read or expressed. Archive, You, Consent, Login and Connect stay on flat paper: an archive should be quiet, and a legal choice is never tinted by how you feel. That restraint is what keeps the field meaning something.

THE SWITCH — IN YOU
Living background

The light behind the app follows your body — it breathes on your heart and takes its colour from the state you are in. It never speeds up when you are stressed; it slows down. Turn it off and everything else works exactly the same.

THE THREE RULES
It never gets faster under stress. The period is monotonic in stress and there is no second layer running at cardiac rate. An earlier draft of this board had one, at 60000/bpm; it was a mirror, not a regulator, and it is gone.
It never goes red. The four hues are the whole range; distress deepens toward violet and indigo and stops there.
It is always switchable, and it degrades before anything else. Reduced motion and low power hold a designed still — same hue, same reach, no movement. Under thermal pressure the second lobe drops first, then the breath, then the field flattens. Layout never changes.
Built on what already ships: advancePulsePhase is already frame-rate-independent and hard-clamped, and the field reuses it for motion. Hue comes from emotionAccent instead of the arousal ramp, so the never-red invariant pinned in tokens.test.ts is untouched.
