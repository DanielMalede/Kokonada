00
Tokens & shell
design/tokens.ts re-authored to the Quiet Instrument palette; one hairline elevation replaces glass; motion tokens gain the 6200 ms stressed breath. Tab bar becomes glyph-first (label under the active tab only); the persistent player sheet lands on every tab root except Generate, which takes a 3px playing hairline instead.
Cross-cutting by nature — this is the one PR that touches every screen. Ship it first or every later diff carries palette noise.
01
Splash
Mark + wordmark on the breathing glow; the 4200 ms brand breath becomes a shared hook the Genesis rings reuse.
Bootsplash asset is native — Android XML and the iOS imageset both need re-export.
02
Onboarding
Three panels — plate, body line, rising arc — each a Skia scene. "No watch needed" sits under the CTA on every panel.
Pager dots need a 44 px hit area around a 26×8 pill, each labelled for screen readers.
03
Login
Three providers at equal weight; submitting becomes a determinate bar, not a spinner; the error names what happened and that nothing was sent.
Apple and Google buttons must be the official assets — compliance gate before merge.
04
Connect services
Two cards, one row shape, REQUIRED against NEVER A GATE. "Try with mood only" becomes a permanent underlined path.
The wearable route must still enter the Art. 9 gate before the OS Health Connect sheet — do not re-order it.
05
Generate — the hero
The body strip moves onto this screen: six-minute trace, bpm / HRV / stillness, one plain sentence. The wheel becomes the plate; the state read card carries a named state, a confidence word and its number, undo, clear and the list alternative. The living field replaces the aura.
The biggest PR. Needs a state name and confidence from the engine; until Wave 4 lands those, read from the existing targets and say less rather than inventing a number.
06
Genesis
Three breathing rings on phase offsets, three status lines, three ticks. Failure silently becomes "Using a set I already trust for this."
Reduced motion and low power hold the rings still with identical layout and copy. Never a spinner.
07
Now Playing
Art, transport, More / Less, and the receipt sentence promoted to a tappable card. Foreign-track and end-of-set get real copy instead of silence.
More / Less needs a feedback endpoint. If it isn't there yet, ship the screen without the pills rather than with dead ones.
08
Mix receipt
New screen. Reads as an argument: what I read → what that set → where it goes → up next, closing on where the data stayed.
The trajectory line needs the arc from the engine. Without it, ship the first two sections only — never a drawn arc that means nothing.
09
Pulse
State headline with confidence, two live tiles, the trace, the whole picture, and the honest empties kept verbatim. Adds the baseline warm-up card.
"Not shared by your watch" stays exactly that — not a dash, not an error, not a nudge to buy a different watch.
10
Archive
Rows split into two intents — play it again, or same state with new music. Nothing scored, nothing expires.
Two 44 px actions per row on a 390 pt screen: check the wrap at the largest Dynamic Type step.
11
You / Vault
One row shape for every connection, the vault panel with consent version and retention, the discovery dial, and the irreversible pair separated below a rule.
The watch shows a pairing code, never a raw token. The discovery dial needs somewhere to persist to.
12
Health consent
Restyled only: static surface, no tint, no motion, eight numbered sections, Decline and Agree identical in every dimension.
Version and date are legal content, not copy — the version bump is a separate decision, not part of a restyle.
13
The living field
The ambient layer lands on Place, Genesis, Now Playing and Pulse — hue from the state, breath from the heart, reach from arousal — and its switch lands in You. Driven by the deriveAuraUniforms that already ships, so this re-skins a hardened driver rather than adding a second one.
Two animated layers across four screens — measure on the S22+ before merging, and keep the drop order under thermal pressure: second lobe, then breath, then flat. There is no beat layer — one was drafted at cardiac rate and cut as a regulator violation.
UNTOUCHED IN EVERY PR
The emotion payload stays at most three placements on a continuous 2-D disc — the plate is a new skin over the same ring buffer.
screenToCircumplex and the wheel geometry keep their maths; only the paint changes.
Every socket and DTO contract, the three-lane state split, and the pinned S- and D-series tests stay as they are.
The arousal ramp still stops at violet. No red enters the emotion range in any token this rebuild introduces.
DECIDE BEFORE PR 05
Five tabs or four. The handoff chose five. With five, NOW is empty for a first-run user — route it to Generate with an honest line rather than an empty player.
Where the state name comes from. Wave 4 produces it. Until then the read card either quotes the existing targets or says less — it never invents a confidence.
The activity presets already match. The app ships the same eight the design shows; the eleven in the README belong to the retired web surface.
Does the field start on? It should — it is the product's whole argument, and turning it off is one tap in You. Starting it off would mean most people never see the thing that makes this app itself.
