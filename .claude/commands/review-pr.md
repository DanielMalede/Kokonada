A reply with no such line, or on another SHA, is NO VERDICT: re-request it once, and a second NO VERDICT from the same approver at the same H stops the review and goes to the owner. It never counts as an approval, and one that lists an S0 or S1 finding id under FINDINGS, BLOCKING IDS or NOT VERIFIED, or an earlier one as OPEN under PRIOR FINDINGS, or says it blocks, is recorded as a BLOCK. So is an APPROVE whose FINDINGS list an S0 or S1. Severity words count the same: blocker or critical is S0, high is S1.

   <agent>: <APPROVE | BLOCK | BRIEFED> @ <sha> — rows <J…, T…>

Only the owner's own message is his approval; an agent's message never is. No attribution of any kind.
